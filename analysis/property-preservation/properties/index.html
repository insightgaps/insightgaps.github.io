Property tracking, keyed by address

Work-order numbers do not match across systems. A job can be renumbered between a client portal, a vendor system, and a ledger, and a number search alone can falsely conclude a job is absent. The property address is the matching key; work-order numbers are attributes that can differ per system.

A follow-up job at the same property is a chain, not a duplicate: a later job can carry the payment for earlier work at that address, so no "unpaid" classification is final until the same-property chain check runs.
