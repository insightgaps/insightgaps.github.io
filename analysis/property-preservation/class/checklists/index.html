Checklists + Templates — Supplementary

SUPPLEMENTARY study aids. These checklists restate the class tools in usable form. They are not exam keys, not scoring rubrics, and not a substitute for the rule sheet on the order in front of you.

1. The Five Questions (every case, in order)

  1. SCOPE — What was actually ordered, in the rule sheet's words?
  2. FACTS — What is verified, what is claimed, what is missing, what contradicts?
  3. PROOF — Does the evidence prove place, time, scope, and clarity?
  4. PERMISSION — Was this action authorised, under this authority, with these conditions?
  5. NEXT — Approve, hold, reject, or escalate — and what is the next action, owner, and time?

2. The Proof Test (every photo)

  • PLACE — Is this photo of this property? Landmarks match?
  • TIME — Does the timestamp, season, and light match the claimed date?
  • SCOPE — Does the photo show the whole job claimed, or only part of it?
  • CLARITY — Can a stranger read the photo and reach the same conclusion?

If any answer is "no" or "cannot tell", the photo does not prove that part of the claim.

3. The Money Ladder (every line)

ALLOWABLE → BID → APPROVED → INVOICED → PAID. For each line, write: quantity × rate × allowance × authorization = audited amount. If any factor is missing, the line is held, not approved.

4. The Triage Card (every queue)

  1. Blocked ≤72 hours first, earliest due first.
  2. Then unblocked ≤72 hours.
  3. Then later.
  4. Ties: unsecured → missed deadline → payment risk.
  5. Check the time-zone strip before reading any deadline.

5. The 4-Line Forensic SBAR (template — fictional example only)

  • STATE: Hold WO-EXAMPLE-001 — do not approve the fictional secure line.
  • EVIDENCE GAP: No dated Before photo for the fictional property; the After photo is cropped.
  • EXPOSURE: Approving it would pay for unproven work and adopt the error.
  • NEXT ACTION: Re-photograph the fictional property, owner: Sample Lead, by Sample Day 2.

6. The Update Line (template — 25 words or fewer)

ORDER: DECISION. Gap (rule). Next step, owner, time.

Fictional example: "WO-EXAMPLE-001: HOLD. No dated Before photo (photo standard). Re-photograph, Sample Lead, Sample Day 2." Count the words. If it is over 25, cut words, not facts.

7. The Decision Log (unscored, every class)

Field Write
First decision Approve / Hold / Reject / Escalate
Confidence 1–3
Reason The one fact it stands on
What changed What flipped or confirmed it after discussion

8. Pre-submission checklist (demo discipline, not a class exam rule)

Complete → report the finished state → owner cross-check → submit ONLY on the owner's word. One job at a time, never on a green light alone. In the demo, there is NO submit action at all — see the demo submission queue.
