Exam + Interview — Hiring Assessment

OWNER GATE — CHOOSE ONE VERSION BEFORE ANY EXAM IS RUN. This page describes TWO incompatible designs: the reconciled proposal below AND Gemini Module 5A. Do NOT run both. Do NOT mix their section maps. Do NOT present either as final until the owner chooses one. Exam length, cut-scores, schedule, and photo medium are also NOT decided on this page — they are owner decisions, marked below.

FICTIONAL training material. This page reconciles three designs found in the verified files. It does NOT merge them into one exam — the owner must choose ONE version before any exam is run. Do not ship more than one version.

Format (reconciled proposal, Presentation Plan Step 4)

  • Individual, open-rules: rules packet allowed (client sheet, triage card, Five Questions card, glossary) — EXCEPT a short closed-book concepts section collected first.
  • Two equivalent forms alternating by seat; candidate codes, not names; calculators allowed; phones away.
  • Sections, 100 points total: concepts 10 · work-order interpretation 20 · evidence review 15 · numerical reasoning 15 · prioritization 10 · integrated practical 30 (unseen case, decision + rationale + next actions + Update Line, 8 points of the 30).
  • Length: TO BE DECIDED BY OWNER. Options in the verified files: 45 minutes (ChatGPT design) · 60 minutes (Gemini Module 5A, see alternative below) · 90 minutes + 15-minute structured interview (Claude design, recommended structure in the Presentation Plan). Section weights stay the same whichever length is chosen.

Alternative design on file — Gemini Module 5A (NOT merged with the above)

  • 60 minutes, 100 points + Critical Controls. Fresh transfer property, Sample Transfer Case (fictional transfer address in the packet), with a Page-1 reference sheet "Investor Guideline Beta-2" containing DELIBERATE habit-trap changes vs the classroom Alpha-1 (personal-property limit, debris rate, winterization rates, secure and boarding rates — exact trap values are in the Module 5A file, not reproduced here as teaching figures).
  • Sections: S1 15 pts (C1, CCV-1) · S2 15 pts (C2, cost-of-delay math) · S3 20 pts (C3, per-photo scoring, CCV-2) · S4 20 pts (C4 triage order + C5 ledger, CCV-3) · S5 30 pts (integrated case: audit + SBAR).
  • Owner must choose: the reconciled proposal above OR Module 5A. Do not run both, do not mix their section maps, and do not present either as final until the owner chooses.

Critical Controls — flags, not auto-fails (all three designs agree)

  1. Close / invoice / approve on missing or contradicted evidence.
  2. Accept / invoice above allowable on verbal approval.
  3. State an unverified claim as fact.
  4. Propose altering / backdating / inventing evidence.

A flag sends the case to structured review. Two unexplained flags = not recommended. Gemini's "minor CCV" loophole is DELETED: a CCV is a CCV. No conditional-hire band for a "minor" flag is used in this programme.

Structured interview (15 minutes, Claude design)

  • Four fixed questions, anchored 1–5 scales, rated BEFORE exam scores are seen: (1) a mistake in your own finished work · (2) contractor says done, photos unclear, deadline in 2 hours · (3) unsure of approval authority · (4) allowable vs approved in two sentences.
  • Interview below 8 = not recommended regardless of exam total (in the Claude design).

Scoring integrity

  • Two raters, calibration on 3 pre-written samples (strong / borderline / weak) before each cohort; differences over 1 point per item or 3 points per section discussed against the key; third reviewer breaks ties.
  • Grammar / handwriting / length never scored. Strong reasoning + weak writing = "coach writing", not rejection. Totals within 5 points treated as equivalent; interview and role fit break ties.
  • Candidate codes, not names. Exams numbered and collected, keys stored separately.

Hiring bands — PROVISIONAL, pilot-gated ONLY

Never publish a band as permanent before a pilot. First-cohort results set the final numbers.

  • Recommend: ≥75 + no flag + interview ≥12 (Claude design, provisional).
  • Consider: 60–74, or 1 explained flag, or interview 8–11.
  • Not recommended: <60, or 2+ flags, or interview <8.
  • Gemini alternative bands (82–100 / 70–81 / 65–69 / <65) are on file in Module 5A and are NOT merged with the above. ChatGPT's position — refuse thresholds until a pilot exists — is recorded as the caution behind the "pilot-gated" label.

Schedule — TO BE DECIDED BY OWNER

  • Single-day intensive (4 hours 15 minutes) vs two-day split, exam after a night's gap. The Presentation Plan recommends two days with a night's gap, because spacing is the best-proven effect in the design — but that recommendation is NOT a decision. Owner decides.

What this page does NOT decide

  • Exam length (above), cut-scores (above), schedule (above), photo medium (annotated diagrams recommended, not decided), which real rule sheets swap in first (owner's call — no real rule sheet is used here).
