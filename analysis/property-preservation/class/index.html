The five work-order types — requirements summary

Every requirement below is typical — always check the order. The order's rulebook governs.

  • Grass Cut + Recut (Session 6): before photos of the full lot; cut + trim + edge; after photos wide (whole lot) + close (edges); date-stamps where the order's rulebook requires them; recut diary set for the next visit. Teams: Field Crew cuts; Vendor Manager dispatches + confirms; Processor proves + invoices; Client Manager submits + tracks the cycle.
  • Initial Secure (Session 6): every exterior lock changed; lockbox code set exactly as the order states; each broken or weak entry boarded; a photo of every lock and every board, before / during / after, labelled. Permission first — occupancy uncertain = HOLD + escalate, no exceptions.
  • Winterization (Session 7): water shut at the main; every line drained; antifreeze in every trap + toilet; water heater handled exactly as the order states; notice posted; BEFORE shots: meter, shutoff, each fixture. Hard rule: system will not hold pressure, or a valve is stuck → do NOT fake it — HOLD + report. A failed test is information, not failure.
  • Debris Removal / Trash-Out (Session 7): before photos of EVERY room; remove only what the order covers (never personal property it does not cover); honest cubic yards — length × width × height in feet ÷ 27 (synthetic example, labelled: 9 × 6 × 3 ft = 162 cu ft ÷ 27 = 6 CY; a 15 CY bill for that pile fails the math); after photos empty + swept.
  • Inspection / Initial Services (Session 7): every room photographed; occupancy signs noted (lived-in signs = STOP + report, do not proceed); damage noted room by room; report written the same day. The report IS the product — the report is the "after" of an inspection.

Every required shot, per type, in one list: Photos List — plus the four photo rules that travel with every set (no photo = no proof; defective shots go back; date-stamps per the order; sets illustrative only).

Money ladder — summary

BID → ALLOWABLE → APPROVED → INVOICED → PAID. Each step is a different person's decision; money is lost between every pair, and every loss has an owner.

Synthetic worked ladder, labelled (same proportions at $1,000 and 100 scale): bid 1,000 → allowable 800 (lost 200: $120 unallowable + $80 unproven, owner the Processor) → approved 700 (lost 100: bid/approval, owner the Vendor Manager's bid + approval) → invoiced 700 (lost 0) → paid 550 (lost 150: $60 unfixed rejection + $50 short-paid + $40 adjustment, owner the Client Manager with the Processor).

Do not confuse this teaching ladder with the fictional $100 → $55 contractor-chain funnel: the funnel is a made-up illustration of fee compression, never a fact, and it is not this ladder.

Loss reason codes, one on every loss: REJECTED (rejected and never fixed), SHORT-PAID (paid below invoice), UNALLOWABLE (scope outside the rulebook), ADJUSTED (absorbed by a property-level adjustment), CHAIN-PAID (paid under a follow-up WO at the same property), UNEXPLAINED (no reason found yet — a queue, never a final answer).

Guards — never teach these

  • Jordan as a national ban (Washington law only, 7 July 2016; $17M settlement, final approval 3 May 2019, taught as the settlement, not the ruling).
  • The $100 → $55 funnel as fact (fictional illustration only).
  • The $5,000 cap as all-inclusive or current (2016 figure, FHA-only; debris, grass, boarding, inspections, pool securing, sump pumps, demolition, registration fees, and utilities are NOT counted in the cap per the current handbook schedule; 7 years covers the Claim Review File, not photos as a standalone rule).
  • Filings as work orders; a combined in-foreclosure + REO number as REO alone; vendor forecasts as facts; real job photos as teaching photos.
