Topic 19 - The Queue + Judgment

About 6 minutes | Page 19 of 20 | All 20 topics

The queue is the list of work orders waiting for a decision. Some are urgent, some can wait, and some should not go ahead at all. Judgement is the skill of telling them apart — and writing the decision down so the next person can act on it.

Three things to take away

  1. First: Triage is not first-come, first-served. Danger and deadline decide the order. A house that is open to the weather comes before a house that is only waiting for a signature, however long the signature has waited.
  2. Second: Approve, hold and reject are three equal decisions. A hold is not a delay — it is a decision with a reason and a next step. Reject, too, is a decision, when the evidence says the work is not needed or not ready.
  3. Third: The Update Line is how a decision travels: one line that says what was decided, by which rule, on what evidence, and what happens next, with an owner and a time. If the line is clear, the next person can act without asking again.
