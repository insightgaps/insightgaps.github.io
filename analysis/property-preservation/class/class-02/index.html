Class 2 — The Permission Gate

FICTIONAL training material. Spine case #EPCS-8841, property 14 Oakfield Street, Maple Heights, OH (synthetic training identifier). "Everything in this class uses a fictional client and fictional rules. Real clients differ. The skill is reading the sheet in front of you."

  • One mental model: No action without applicable authority, scope, and approval conditions.
  • Exit skill: Identify when to proceed, hold, or escalate because authority is uncertain.
  • Callback (60 seconds, not a retelling): Act 3, the reckoning — authority first.
  • Tools in this class: the Epistemic Filter (Verified Fact / Unverified Claim / Missing Prerequisite / Contradictory Evidence) + the Five Questions, SCOPE and PERMISSION first.

The 30-minute run

Time Block What happens
0:00–0:03 Cold verdict on an unseen file One new file, photo only. Verdict: Approve / Hold / Reject + the one fact it stands on. Everyone writes; no discussion yet.
0:03–0:08 Cold-call defence One learner defends in 90 seconds, from evidence only. Then a second learner with the opposite verdict. No trainer verdict yet.
0:08–0:15 Socratic pressure "What assumption is your verdict standing on? What fact would flip it? Who benefits if you're wrong?"
0:15–0:23 Paired live audit (the foil) Pairs audit the foil case together, then each writes an individual verdict + one-line Update Line (25 words or fewer: ORDER: DECISION. Gap (rule). Next step, owner, time).
0:23–0:27 Re-vote + expert reveal Everyone re-votes. Trainer reveals the expert verdict and narrates the expert's reasoning path, naming the rule and the exact evidence.
0:27–0:30 Transfer bridge One question for next time, no summary lecture: "Next class, same house — the evidence itself is on trial. What will you watch for?"

Lessons

  1. Occupancy is a fact to prove, not a claim to accept. The FTV Three-Prong Test in the fictional Alpha-1 rule sheet: utilities off / personal-property threshold / no recent habitation signals. On conflict, the mandatory action applies.
  2. Pre-foreclosure securing and post-foreclosure REO are different authorities. The same lock change can be unlawful in one and lawful in the other. That difference decides this class's cases.
  3. A completed action does not ratify itself. Approving a bad file does not close it — it adopts it. This is the Sunk-Action Ratification Trap, taught here through the chain (ASSIGN → SCOPE → DO → PROVE → APPROVE → GET PAID).
  4. Jordan, bounded: 2016 Washington Supreme Court ruling that a lock change is unlawful possession under Washington law, regardless of purported vacancy; $17M settlement approved 2019. Never "U.S. Supreme Court precedent", never a national rule, never a fee or damage figure beyond what the verified files state.
  5. Lien vs Title theory matters — because who holds what right decides who may authorise entry. Teach rule-identification + hold/escalate, not a 30-minute property-law course.

Case: WO-101 (spine, fictional)

  • Claim: contractor rekeyed the property and winterized it. Winterization claimed at a fictional amount in the case packet; the packet's gold-standard escalation is RED — REJECT INVOICE ($0 paid in the fictional packet) + legal/occupancy escalation, with a 4-Line SBAR to the Servicer Occupancy Desk.
  • Evidence against the claim: fridge running at a cold temperature, clock lit, prescription only days old — habitation signals contradicting vacancy.
  • Filter verdict: Verified Fact (power on, fresh medicine) vs Unverified Claim (vacancy) vs Missing Prerequisite (authority to enter) vs Contradictory Evidence (claim vs signals). Anchor ABT: "The borrower still owns it AND the contractor rekeyed it, BUT the power was on and medicine was fresh, THEREFORE reject and escalate."
  • Foil — WO-101B (fictional foil property): post-foreclosure REO, Trustee's Deed, Sheriff's Return of Writ, meter zero → GREEN — approve the fictional Initial Secure amount in the packet. Same action, different authority, opposite verdict. That contrast IS the lesson.

Workbook / tasks

  • Pre-read (flipped): WO-101 packet + Alpha-1 §1 (Legal Authority & Occupancy) + one question the night before: "What is your verdict, and what single fact decides it?"
  • In class: cold verdict (written, ungraded) · cold-call defence · paired foil audit · individual Update Line (≤25 words) · re-vote.
  • Retrieval (closed-book): name the four Epistemic Filter categories + state the pre-foreclosure vs REO difference in one sentence each.
  • Decision Log: first decision, confidence 1–3, reason, what changed.
