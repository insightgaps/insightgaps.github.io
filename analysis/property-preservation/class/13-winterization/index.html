Topic 13 - Winterization

About 6 minutes | Page 13 of 20 | All 20 topics

Winterization is protecting a house water and heating systems from freezing. Done wrong, a pipe can burst in the night and ruin a house before morning. There are three types, and your teacher names them live in the session — they are not written on this page.

Three things to take away

  1. First: Read the order. The order tells you what the system expects, which parts of the house it covers, and what the last record says about the system. Start with the paper, not with the pipes.
  2. Second: Read the system. Look at the pipes, the heating, the water supply, and what is already protected. A system that looks ready may not be ready — check it the way the order asks you to check it, not the way it looks from the doorway.
  3. Third: Read the climate. The freeze risk in the place and the season decides how far the protection must go. The same house needs different protection in a mild winter and a hard one, and the climate around it is part of the job.
