Reading List — Supplementary, With Caveats

Read the caveat first, or this list will mislead you. In the verified Module 1 digest, only ONE citation is recorded as live-verified by Muse: Carpenter / Pan / Butler (2022) on retrieval + spacing. The other citations below are as cited in the verified training files — plausible and well-formed, but NOT live-checked in those files. Treat them as "as cited in the programme files", not as independently verified bibliography, and check a citation before teaching it as a fact.

Learning science (as cited)

  • Retrieval + spacing — Carpenter / Pan / Butler (2022) + Dunlosky (2013). Carpenter 2022: live-verified in the programme files.
  • Pretesting / prediction — Kornell, Hays & Bjork (2009). As cited, not live-checked.
  • Worked examples / cognitive load / expertise reversal — Kalyuga et al. (2003). As cited, not live-checked.
  • Interleaving / contrasting cases — Rohrer (2012). As cited, not live-checked.
  • Psychological safety vs evaluation standardization — Edmondson (1999). As cited, not live-checked.

Assessment (as cited, with a modern caveat)

  • Work samples — OPM guidance + Schmidt & Hunter (1998). Caveat, verified in the programme files: Schmidt & Hunter's headline validities were revised downward by Sackett et al. (2022). Cite the newer numbers, not 1998 alone. The design conclusion (job-relevant work samples) still stands.

Economics / systems (as cited)

  • Principal-agent moral hazard — Holmström (1979); Eisenhardt (1989). As cited, not live-checked. Labelled "Supported Analytical Framework" in the programme files, not measured preservation data.
  • Cost of Poor Quality / 1–10–100 — Juran (1988), applied via HUD ML 2016-02. Caveat: the 1–10–100 multipliers are a widely repeated quality-management heuristic, not a measured preservation-specific multiplier. Never teach them as EPCS data.
  • Queueing / Little's Law — Little (1961); Reinertsen (2009) on cost of delay. As cited, not live-checked.

Forensic reasoning (as cited)

  • Premature closure / confirmation bias — Croskerry (2003). As cited, not live-checked.
  • Swiss Cheese / active vs latent failures — Reason (2000). As cited, not live-checked. Caveat in the programme files: preservation is not life-safety, so forcing functions must be lightweight (15–30 seconds per work order).

Regulations and cases (teaching boundaries)

  • HUD ML 2016-02 (5 February 2016, FHA-only): $5,000 maximum P&P allowance, dated/labeled before/after photos. The "7 years" figure is claim-file retention, not a photo rule.
  • Jordan v. Nationstar: 2016 Washington Supreme Court (Washington law only); $17M settlement approved 2019. Never "U.S. Supreme Court precedent", never a national rule.
  • CFPB Regulation X, 12 CFR 1024.38 (policies-and-procedures rule). A "5-day" figure sometimes attached to it in programme drafts resembles a different error-resolution timeline — verify before teaching any day count.
  • GAO-12-34 (2011): vacant-property context. Specific percentages in programme drafts are checkable, not yet checked — do not teach them as verified figures.

What this list is for

Background reading for the trainer, not required reading for candidates. Candidates need the case packet, the rule sheet, and the tools — not this bibliography. If a citation cannot be checked, teach the TOOL (retrieval, Proof Test, Triage Card) and cite the tool's programme source, not the unverified paper.
