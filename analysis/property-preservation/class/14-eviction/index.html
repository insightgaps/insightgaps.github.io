Topic 14 - Eviction

About 6 minutes | Page 14 of 20 | All 20 topics

An eviction work order is different from the other five jobs. It comes after a legal process, and the preservation crew job is not that process — it is what comes after it: secure the house, clear what the order says to clear, photograph everything, and record it all.

Three things to take away

  1. First: An eviction order follows a court process, and the crew must not act before the process allows it. If the date or the paper is not right, stop — and ask. Acting early is not helping; it is taking a risk with someone else home.
  2. Second: Treat the property, and anything left behind, exactly as the order instructs. Do not improvise, do not decide for yourself what should be kept or cleared, and do not take anything that is not yours to take.
  3. Third: Photograph everything first, before anything is moved. The first photograph is the record of what was there. Once it is moved, that record is gone, and no one can put it back.
