Topic 2 - The Chain

About 6 minutes | Page 2 of 20 | All 20 topics

No one person looks after a vacant house. It passes along a chain: the owner, the lender, the servicer, the inspector, the preservation office and the crew. Each person in the chain sees only their own piece of the house, so the record has to travel with the house, from hand to hand.

Three things to take away

  1. First: No one in the chain sees the whole house. The inspector sees the condition, the office sees the paper, the crew sees the work in front of them. If the record is missing, each person works blind.
  2. Second: A broken link stops the whole chain. A missing photograph, an unsigned note, a work order that never arrived — any one of these can leave a house waiting for weeks while everyone assumes someone else has it.
  3. Third: Your job is to make the job of the next person possible, not just your own. Write the note you would want to read, take the photograph you would want to see, and leave the record in the place the next person will look.
