Topic 7 - The System Today

About 6 minutes | Page 7 of 20 | All 20 topics

Today the system is a network, not a single company. A work order can pass through many hands — a national company, a regional office, a local crew — and the software keeps the record while people make the decisions.

Three things to take away

  1. First: Today the system is a network. A work order is created in one place, checked in another, and carried out by a crew that may never meet the person who wrote it. The network works only when the record travels with the order.
  2. Second: Software keeps the record, but people still decide. The system shows you the house, its history and its deadline — it does not judge the house for you. That judgement is your job, and it is the reason you are being trained.
  3. Third: Your first week in the system is for learning its rhythm: what is due today, what is late, what is waiting for a decision, and who to ask when you do not know. Learn the rhythm before you try to hurry it.
