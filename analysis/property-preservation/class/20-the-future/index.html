Topic 20 - The Future

About 6 minutes | Page 20 of 20 | All 20 topics

Where is preservation going? The tools will change — better photographs, better software, better training — but the core job will not. A vacant house will still need to be kept safe, proved and recorded, until it is a home again.

Three things to take away

  1. First: Tools change, the question does not: is this house safe, is the work proved, and is the record clear? Any new tool that helps you answer that question is worth learning. Any tool that does not is a distraction.
  2. Second: The future belongs to people who can read a house and write it down clearly. Software can hold a record, and a camera can take a photograph, but neither of them can decide what a house needs. That is your work, and it is not going away.
  3. Third: This class ends here. Your learning does not. The next house will teach you something this course could not — go and look at it properly, and write down what you find.
