Photos List — Every Required Shot, Per Work-Order Type

DEMO / TRAINING ONLY — not a real work system. Every case, client, address, amount, and rule sheet in this class area is FICTIONAL training material, unless a line is explicitly marked as real industry history. Real clients differ. The skill is reading the sheet in front of you.

Every requirement below is typical — always check the order. The order's rulebook governs: what it demands, you photograph; what it does not demand, you do not assume.

The four photo rules (they travel with every set)

  1. No photo = no proof. An unphotographed lock is an unlocked door. A room with no before photo cannot be billed.
  2. Defective shots go back to the field. Blurry, dark, missing-room, missing-fixture, wrong-property, or unlabelled shots are send-backs — never papered over.
  3. Date-stamps per the order. Date-stamps are required by some rulebooks, not by others — always check the order. If the order is unclear, date-stamp anyway: a date-stamped photo is never wrong, but a required photo without a date-stamp is unpaid.
  4. Sets are illustrative only. The photo sets in this course are public photographs of the work type — not job photos, not one job's sequence, never real job photos.

1. Grass Cut + Recut

  • BEFORE: full-lot photos of the overgrown lawn, so anyone can see where the job started.
  • DURING: the crew at work — mower running, trimmer in hand, the cut line half done.
  • AFTER: wide photos of the whole lot, plus close photos of the neat edges and clean lines. A striped, neat lawn in the after photo is the signature of a good cut.
  • Date-stamps: where the order's rulebook requires them.
  • Recut: the same set repeats on the order's cycle, with the recut diary set for the next visit.

2. Initial Secure — the strictest proof in the course

  • BEFORE: the old lock and the broken entry, every entry.
  • DURING: the rekeying and the boarding happening.
  • AFTER: the new lock and the firm board, every entry.
  • Rule: a photo of EVERY lock and EVERY board, before / during / after, labelled. If one lock has no after photo, the secure is not proven. If one board has no photo, the board might as well not exist.
  • Permission first: occupancy uncertain = HOLD + escalate, no exceptions — before any secure photo is taken.

3. Winterization

  • BEFORE shots: the meter, the shutoff valve, and each fixture, photographed before the work.
  • DURING: each step photographed as it happens — shutoff, drain, antifreeze.
  • AFTER: dry fixtures, antifreeze visible in traps, winterization notice posted.
  • Why shot (teaching context): frozen pipes — the bulge before the burst, the damage this job prevents.
  • Send-back rule: a missing fixture photo sends the job back. A failed pressure test is reported honestly (HOLD + report), never faked and never photographed as "done."

4. Debris Removal / Trash-Out

  • BEFORE: every room, exactly as found, full and messy. A room with no before photo cannot be billed.
  • DURING: the cleanout in progress — crew, bags, piles leaving. The pile is measured, not guessed.
  • AFTER: every room, empty and swept. The after photo is the proof the job is done.
  • Measurement rule: honest cubic yards — length × width × height in feet, divided by 27. Synthetic example, labelled: 9 × 6 × 3 ft = 162 cu ft ÷ 27 = 6 CY. A 15 CY bill for that pile fails the math.

5. Inspection / Initial Services

  • DURING: the inspector at work — every room photographed, every corner checked. An inspection that misses a room is a report that lies by omission.
  • AFTER = the written report. The report is the product: rooms, systems, damage, written down room by room, the same day. There is no other "after" for an inspection.
  • Send-back rule: a blurry photo, a dark photo, or a missing room means send-back. A report with a hole in it is not a report.
  • STOP rule: if occupancy signs appear mid-inspection, STOP + report. The report is never worth a person.
