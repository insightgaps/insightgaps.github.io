Class 4 — The Money Trail

FICTIONAL training material. All figures in this class are synthetic and labeled as such — never market facts, never real client figures. Spine case #EPCS-8841, property 14 Oakfield Street, Maple Heights, OH (synthetic training identifier). "Everything in this class uses a fictional client and fictional rules. Real clients differ. The skill is reading the sheet in front of you."

  • One mental model: Claimed, allowable, approved, invoiced, and paid are five different stages.
  • Exit skill: Reconcile a claim to its source: quantity × rate × allowance × authorization.
  • Callback (60 seconds, not a retelling): Act 2 / Act 4 scale economics — why volume without control loses money.
  • Tools in this class: the Money Ladder (ALLOWABLE → BID → APPROVED → INVOICED → PAID) + volumetric math + the over-allowable cap.

The 30-minute run

Same template as every class: cold verdict → cold-call defence → Socratic pressure → paired foil audit → re-vote + expert reveal → transfer bridge. Scaffolding in Sessions 3–4: trainer models only the first step; learners audit the rest.

Lessons

  1. Billed ≠ earned; only APPROVED becomes PAID. Every other stage is a claim, not money.
  2. Volumetric math is the first audit: volume in cubic feet ÷ 27 = cubic yards. Worked example in the packet: a pile measured 9 × 6 × 3 feet = 162 cubic feet = 6 cubic yards. Billed at 15 cubic yards, that is 2.5× actual — 150% overbilling — before any rate is even discussed.
  3. Bundling hides over-allowable lines. In the packet, a lockbox amount billed inside a flat secure line must be unbundled before the line can be judged. If a line is over the allowable, the over-allowable cap proof decides it — verbal approval does not.
  4. Dimensional and bundling illusion traps: the eye accepts a big number; the audit accepts only quantity × rate × allowance × authorization, line by line.
  5. One unrecovered payout wipes out many good orders. The packet's foil states the lesson as: one fictional $220 unrecovered payout wipes out the profit of 22 routine fictional $100 orders. That is a teaching illustration, not a measured market ratio.

Case: WO-104 (spine, fictional)

  • Claim: a 4-line ledger, unaudited vs audited reconciliation. Line 2 carries the Over-Allowable Cap Proof. A lockbox amount is bundled inside a flat secure line and must be unbundled.
  • Anchor: billed ≠ earned; only APPROVED becomes PAID.
  • Foil — WO-104B, supplemental ledger (fictional): claimed fictional amounts vs audited fictional amounts, with a protected net gross margin stated in the packet. Same ledger shape, different audit outcome. That contrast IS the lesson.

Workbook / tasks

  • In class: cold verdict · cold-call defence · paired foil audit · individual Update Line (≤25 words) · re-vote.
  • Retrieval (closed-book): audit one ledger line cold — quantity, rate, allowance, authorization — and state the audited amount in one line.
  • Calculator rule: calculators allowed in the exam; arithmetic slips earn partial credit there, blind acceptance earns none. See the exam page.
