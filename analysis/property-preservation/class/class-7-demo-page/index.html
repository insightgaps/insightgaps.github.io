08 Winter, Debris, and Inspection

Winterization, debris clearing, and exterior inspections require objective scientific metrics rather than subjective visual guesses.

⚙ PPW MOCK / WINTERIZATION & DEBRIS AUDIT SHEET · 100% SYNTHETIC
KEY POINT 1

Winterization Pressure Gauge Integrity

Systems must hold 35 PSI of dry compressed air for 30 minutes with the main shut off. Antifreeze goes into all traps, toilets, and fixtures.

KEY POINT 2

Debris Volume Arithmetic

Debris claims must follow cubic-yard geometry (L × W × H in feet ÷ 27). Subjective guesses are rejected without tape measure photo evidence.

KEY POINT 3

Exterior & Interior Inspection Rigor

Inspectors do not execute repairs; they document physical roof, foundation, electrical, and plumbing conditions without bias.

Governing Rules & Compliance Standards

The 35 PSI Integrity Mandate

Pressure gauge needle must hold steady at 35 PSI; zero drop permitted. Two photos required showing start and end timestamps (minimum 30 min duration).

Cubic Yard Geometry Standard

Debris volume = (Length in feet × Width in feet × Height in feet) ÷ 27. Every debris invoice must include dimensional tape-measure photos.

Non-Toxic Antifreeze Mandate

Use strictly non-toxic RV/marine propylene glycol (pink); automotive ethylene glycol is strictly prohibited by EPA guidelines.

Operational Requirements & Dealing Protocols

Winterization Warning Notice

Tape signed and dated high-visibility warning stickers to every toilet bowl, shower valve, and main breaker box.

Disposal Facility Ticket

Attach certified transfer station or landfill scale receipts for all debris billed beyond 4 cubic yards.

Dealing Point — Discovered Frozen Pipes

If supply lines have already burst and thawed ice is leaking, shut main stop immediately, photograph damage before touching, and phone emergency dispatch for flood mitigation bid.
