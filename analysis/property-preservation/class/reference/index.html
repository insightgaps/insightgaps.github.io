What is verified core vs supplementary — at a glance

  • VERIFIED CORE: the six class pages, the exam page, and the deck structures — taken from the verified training files, with incomplete parts marked "to be completed by owner".
  • SUPPLEMENTARY: glossary, checklists/templates, practice cases, FAQs, reading list, study guide, advanced edge cases (practice-guidance parts labelled as such), and this reference index. Supplementary material helps you learn and find things fast; it never sets an exam answer, a cut-score, a schedule, or a hiring decision.
  • FAKE everywhere it must be: every demo figure, every practice case figure, and every sample in the demo is FAKE/SAMPLE and labelled as such. Training case figures (WO-101 → WO-105) are FICTIONAL training figures, not real figures.
