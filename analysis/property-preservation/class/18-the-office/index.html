Topic 18 - The Office

About 6 minutes | Page 18 of 20 | All 20 topics

The office is where a job becomes a record. The office engine — PPW — is the system where the office prepares the work, and it is not the client portal: the office prepares, the client receives. A bid is written in the office, and it is a promise made on paper.

Three things to take away

  1. First: A bid is written for a person who will decide without visiting the house. The scope must be complete on paper: what will be done, where, and how it will be proved. If the reader has to guess, the bid is not finished.
  2. Second: Every line in a bid needs a photograph behind it. No photograph, no line. A scope that cannot be shown cannot be defended, and a bid that cannot be defended will not be approved.
  3. Third: The PPW is the office memory. The photographs, the report and the bid are entered there, tagged to their line items, and filed before, during and after. If it is not on the PPW, the office does not know it.
