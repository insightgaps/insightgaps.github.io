Topic 4 - The Flood

About 6 minutes | Page 4 of 20 | All 20 topics

One vacant house is a job. A thousand vacant houses at once is a flood. When a flood comes, the system has too many properties, too few crews and too little time — and the way you work has to change.

Three things to take away

  1. First: A flood is a problem of volume, not of one house. The question is no longer what this house needs, but which houses need attention first, and which can wait a week without getting worse.
  2. Second: In a flood, the houses that get attention first are the ones whose danger is clearest: an open door, broken glass, water running, a roof that will not last the season. Clear danger beats a tidy lawn.
  3. Third: Records matter more, not less, when volume rises. A flood without records is a mess no one can untangle afterwards, and the houses that are missed are the ones no one wrote down.
