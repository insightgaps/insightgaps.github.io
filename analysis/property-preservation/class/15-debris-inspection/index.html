Topic 15 - Debris + Inspection

About 6 minutes | Page 15 of 20 | All 20 topics

Debris and inspection go together: one clears a house, and the other looks at it properly. A house that is cleared but not inspected is a house no one has really seen.

Three things to take away

  1. First: Debris is not rubbish to you — it is evidence until the order says otherwise. Remove what the order says to remove, sort it as the order asks, and photograph what you found before you clear it.
  2. Second: An inspection is a walk with a purpose: the same route and the same checks, every time. Walk the house in a fixed order, note the damage, note the hazards, and photograph each one where it stands.
  3. Third: The inspection report is the next crew starting point. Write it for someone who has never seen the house: where the damage is, what it looks like, and what it needs. If they cannot find it from your report, the report has failed.
