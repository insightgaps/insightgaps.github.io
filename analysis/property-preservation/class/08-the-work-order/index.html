Topic 8 - The Work Order

About 6 minutes | Page 8 of 20 | All 20 topics

The work order is the house instruction and its record, in one document. It has eleven stops, and a job is not finished until every stop is passed. Skip a stop, and the order is incomplete — even if the work itself is done.

Three things to take away

  1. First: The eleven stops are: intake, assignment, the permission gate, dispatch, arrival with before photographs, the work itself, during photographs, after photographs, photo quality check, submission, and reconciliation with close-out. Each stop has its own job.
  2. Second: The order is the proof, and the work and the record travel together. The before photographs prove what you found, the during photographs prove what you did, and the after photographs prove what you left behind.
  3. Third: A stuck order is usually stuck at one stop. Find the stop, and you find the problem: a missing photograph, a decision waiting, a record not yet checked. Fix the stop, and the order moves again.
