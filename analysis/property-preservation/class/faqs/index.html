Class FAQs — Supplementary

SUPPLEMENTARY study aid. Answers below restate the verified delivery system in plain form. Where the verified files leave a decision open, the answer says so — it does not fill the gap.

How does a class actually run?

Thirty minutes, same template every time: cold verdict (3 min) → cold-call defence (5 min) → Socratic pressure (7 min) → paired foil audit (8 min) → re-vote + expert reveal (4 min) → transfer bridge (3 min). The trainer talks at most about 8 minutes, except Class 1 (about 10 minutes, by owner order). Learners talk, argue, and decide for the rest.

Is classroom work graded?

No. Classroom predictions are never graded, and the Decision Log is unscored. Hiring is judged only via the anonymously graded post-course exam + structured interview, under the scoring-integrity rules on the exam page. Wrong answers in class, when reasoned, are praised — bluffing is the only failure.

Do I need to memorise fee schedules or portal steps?

No. The exam is a synthetic packet with all rules and allowables printed in it. What is examined is judgment: verdict + rationale + next action, from the packet in front of you. Live portals, memorised fee schedules, and lien negotiation are explicitly excluded from the exam in the verified files.

What should I bring / read before each class?

The next work order + rule sheet + one question, sent the night before: "What is your verdict, and what single fact decides it?" Class opens with verdicts already formed. The glossary is a reference sheet, not a lecture.

What if I disagree with the expert verdict?

Defend it in class — learners address each other, not just the trainer, and attack ideas, not people. After class, write the disagreement into your Decision Log with the fact it stands on. A reasoned disagreement that survives cross-examination is learning; an undefended verdict is not.

Can I use this hub after the class ends?

Yes — that is the point of the reference page. Come back months later, find the tool (Five Questions, Proof Test, Money Ladder, Triage Card, SBAR, Update Line) in under a minute, and apply it to the order in front of you. The hub is a handbook, not just a lesson sequence.

Is the training case real?

No. Case #EPCS-8841, all work orders, all amounts, and all rule sheets in the class area are FICTIONAL training material, except where a line is explicitly marked as real industry history (a court ruling, a published regulation). Real clients differ. The skill is reading the sheet in front of you.

What is still not decided?

Exam length, cut-scores, schedule format, photo medium, and which real rule sheets swap in first — see the pending-decisions table on the class hub. Do not treat any of those as decided because a FAQ mentions an option.
