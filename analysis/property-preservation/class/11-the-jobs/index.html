Topic 11 - The Jobs

About 6 minutes | Page 11 of 20 | All 20 topics

All preservation work comes down to six jobs: grass, secure, winterization, debris, inspection and eviction. Different jobs, different tools, different proof — but one purpose: keep a vacant house safe, legal and ready to be a home again.

Three things to take away

  1. First: Each job has its own proof. The photographs that prove a grass cut do not prove a secure, and the photographs that prove a secure do not prove a winterization. Learn what proves each job, and take those photographs every time.
  2. Second: Jobs are ordered by need, not by habit. The most urgent damage comes first: a house that is open to the weather or to strangers comes before a house that only needs its lawn cut.
  3. Third: No job is small. A missed grass cut can become a complaint from the street, a missed secure can become a break-in, and a missed inspection can become a house no one knows is damaged. Small jobs are how big problems are prevented.
