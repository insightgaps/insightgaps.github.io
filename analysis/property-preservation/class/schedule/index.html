Whole Class Schedule — 10 Hours

DRAFT schedule index, for the owner's check. Master source: the 10-hour schedule in the hiring-training files (CLASS_SCHEDULE_10HOUR_COMPLETE.md). That schedule supersedes the earlier 6-day / 6-hour plan as the master schedule.

The master rule, from the schedule itself: 10 hours, in 10 sessions of 60 minutes = 600 minutes. If any session needs more time, extend it — the total grows past 10 hours, nothing gets cut. Teacher: Anik teaches himself. Muse prepares; the teacher tells and decides. The teacher decides how much of each session to teach, and how deep to go — the schedule does not decide for him. Audience: fresh graduate trainees (future experienced-people training draws from the same whole).

House thread, all 10 sessions: born, emptied, served, ordered, billed, judged, sold to a new family — the story restarts. Every session ends with a decision, not a summary. Each session has a different hook, a different task, and a different memory anchor. No two sessions repeat a format.

How to use this index: Session 1 is teach-ready in draft below. Sessions 2–10 are outlines only — a map of what each session holds, not the session itself. Teach from the Session 1 page, not from an outline.

GATE — read this before teaching anything from this index

Session outlines (Sessions 2–10 — outlines only, NOT full sessions)

Session 2 — The Peaks + The Loop (outline only)

Retrieval of Session 1 from memory, no notes. The two peaks, never collapsed: TOTAL starts peak 1972 = 2,379,000 (1,309,000 single-family + 1,070,000 multifamily; multifamily-led, 1968 Housing Act goal, Section 236 subsidies, then the 1973 moratorium and the fall to 1,353,000 in 1974); SINGLE-FAMILY peak 2005 = 1,716,000 (total 2,068,000; average 30-year rate 5.87% in 2005, loosened/subprime lending, securitisation, speculation); trough 2009 = 554,000; starts 2024 = 1,365,000. The economy loop as a loop (jobs/income → ability to pay; rates → how much house the same payment buys; credit standards + programmes → who can borrow; building → supply/jobs/prices), then the break: price fall + payment shock → default. Cliffhanger + task: the family stops paying; pairs draw the loop from memory and mark where it breaks. The break is next session. Full session NOT built — outline only, pending the teacher naming it.

Session 3 — The Fall: From Missed Payment to Vacant House (outline only)

Retrieval of the loop + the break, from memory. The foreclosure road in order: default → loss mitigation FIRST (forbearance, modification, short sale, deed-in-lieu; federal floor generally no foreclosure start until over 120 days delinquent — verify current text) → foreclosure by state law (judicial or non-judicial) → auction → no buyer tops the lender's credit bid → REO or conveyance to the insuring agency. A filing is NOT a repossessed home. Vacancy decay: week 1 signals vacancy; weeks 2–4 water and weather work on it; month 2 damp becomes mold; month 3 onward vandalism and stripping. Card order task: pairs put the shuffled road cards in order for THIS house. Cliffhanger: nobody is paid to care about her — yet. Who steps in? Full session NOT built — outline only, pending the teacher naming it.

Session 4 — The System: Nationals, Proof, Rules (outline only)

Retrieval of the foreclosure road, no notes. Outsourcing is structural (servicers cannot staff 50 states); Safeguard, bought/renamed 1990, is the documented historical industry EXAMPLE only — never the founder, "largest" unverified company marketing. Proof evolution, hands-on: three photos, three eras (physical mail, real camera, portal) — trainees spot what is missing in each. Date-stamps: required by some clients, not by others — always check the order. Rules courtroom: Jordan (Washington Supreme Court, 7 July 2016 — Washington law ONLY, never a national ban; later federal settlement $17M, final approval 3 May 2019) and Mortgagee Letter ML 2016-02 (5 Feb 2016, FHA-insured loans only; 2016 dollars — re-check current figures). Rule card: "Permission first. Proof always." Then the first work order arrives. Full session NOT built — outline only, pending the teacher naming it.

Session 5 — The Order: One Work Order, Start to Finish (outline only)

Retrieval of the rule card + the three photos, from memory. The lifecycle on the wall map: order → intake → permission gate → dispatch → field → proof → QC → invoice → submission → payment → follow-up. Permission gate: occupancy uncertain → HOLD + escalate, no exceptions. Decision lab: synthetic WO for their house (labelled), Secure + Grass Cut, but fridge cold, clock lit, prescription 4 days old — approve, hold, or reject? Pairs decide + write an Update Line (25 words or fewer). Expert reveal: HOLD + escalate. Update Line check (all four must pass): occupancy signal named, decision stated, next step named, 25 words or fewer. Classroom predictions are never graded — reasoning is. Full session NOT built — outline only, pending the teacher naming it.

Session 6 — The Work I: Grass + Secure (outline only)

Retrieval of the lifecycle + the gate, from memory. Grass Cut + Recut: cut, trim, edge, clear clippings; initial = first overgrown cut; recut = repeat cycle; required (typical, check the order): before photos of the full lot, cut + trim + edge, after photos wide + close, date-stamps where the order's rulebook requires them. Initial Secure: change every exterior lock, set the lockbox code exactly as the order states, board broken/weak entries; secure first — nothing happens in an open house; a photo of every lock + every board, before/during/after, labelled. Decision + close: trainees judge one grass photo and one secure photo — done or not done, with the missing shot named. Full session NOT built — outline only, pending the teacher naming it.

Session 7 — The Work II: Winterization + Debris + Inspection (outline only)

Retrieval of grass + secure "done" states, from memory. Winterization: shut off, drain every line, antifreeze every trap; HARD RULE — system will not hold pressure, or a valve is stuck → do NOT fake it — HOLD + report; a failed test is information, not failure. Debris Removal / Trash-Out: remove what the order states until empty + swept; measured in cubic yards (length × width × height in feet ÷ 27); synthetic example (labelled): 9 × 6 × 3 ft = 162 cu ft ÷ 27 = 6 CY; a 15 CY bill for that pile fails the math. Inspection / Initial Services: look at everything, write exactly what you find; no work, just truth; the report IS the product; lived-in signs = STOP + report. Full session NOT built — outline only, pending the teacher naming it.

Session 8 — Damage + Property: Read Any House (outline only)

Retrieval of winterization steps + CY math, from memory. Damage types in story-then-skill order: water, mold, vandalism, weather, neglect — what each does, what it looks like, what it means for the next job. Property knowledge: roof, plumbing, HVAC, electrical, structure; age matters (median owner-occupied home age 42 in 2024; ~47% built pre-1980); region + season matter. Assessment skill, in order: identify, document, quantify — safety first (never enter an unsafe structure alone; standing water + electricity treated as live danger; animals, squatters, or weapons signs = leave and escalate). Decision + close: given one damage photo, verdict what this house needs first — safety, then proof, then work. Full session NOT built — outline only, pending the teacher naming it.

Session 9 — Money + Workflows: Bidding, Invoice, Three Roles (outline only)

Retrieval of identify/document/quantify, from memory. Money ladder: ALLOWABLE → BID → APPROVED → INVOICED → PAID; each step is a different person's decision; money is lost between every pair (synthetic example, labelled: claimed 100 → allowable 80 → approved 70 → invoiced 70 → paid 55, every bleed mapped to an owner). Bid construction: scope from the order + rate from the governing rulebook + evidence from the field = the bid; no scope, no rate, or no evidence means no bid. Over-allowable (synthetic, labelled): approval via the claim portal (P260) at least 5 business days before the conveyance due date (verify current schedule). Gross = net on these orders (no client discount) — always check the order, because discount rules differ by client. Three workflows: Vendor Manager (dispatch + gate), Processor (prove + invoice; the owner marks Ready For Office himself — the Processor never marks it), Client Manager (submit in the client portal, track review, follow-up WO chains by address, ~60-day wait, loss reason codes REJECTED / SHORT-PAID / UNALLOWABLE / ADJUSTED / CHAIN-PAID / UNEXPLAINED, property-level adjustments owner-approved, unexplained driven to zero). Full session NOT built — outline only, pending the teacher naming it.

Session 10 — Money's End + Judgment: The Capstone (outline only)

Retrieval of the whole journey as one chain — trainees rebuild the wall map from memory, Sessions 1 to 9, no notes. Money's end: rejections (fix + resubmit + evidence, never argue without proof) → the 60-day wait (tracking, not panic) → payment matching → loss reasons → property-level adjustments → every loss gets a reason; unexplained is a queue driven to zero. Unseen case, unaided (25–50 min): a new synthetic case, never seen before, no help — decide (approve / hold / reject) + defend in writing + Update Line (25 words or fewer); this is the graduation proof, not memory. A graded hiring exam attaches later only if the teacher asks for it. Graduation: the lifecycle as a checklist, owned by the class; the house is sold to a new family — the story restarts. Full session NOT built — outline only, pending the teacher naming it.

If a session needs more time — extend, never cut

Any session may run to 90 minutes; the schedule then totals more than 10 hours, which the teacher's own rule allows. Never cut to fit the clock: cut discussion first, never the decision, never the retrieval, never the history. If a session still does not fit, split it across two days and keep going — the session count grows, the content does not shrink. The teacher decides how much of each session to teach on any given day.

How to teach it — professor, corporate professional, trainer (all sessions)

One teacher, three modes, in every session. Professor: frame the concept + the system + the why; precise terms, defined on first use; Socratic questions; real anchors cited as evidence, never trivia. Corporate professional: show the business stake — what it costs, what it earns, what breaks if done wrong, what the client sees; crisp, outcome-first, every claim sourced, every decision owned. Trainer: make them DO it — photos to judge, cases to decide, lines to write, checklists to use; practice + feedback, never lecture alone; praise reasoned wrong answers, correct only bluffing and guessing; plain English always; no one reads bullet points aloud, ever. Rhythm: opening story/history beat as professor → photos, numbers, workflows as professional proof → pair task + decision as trainer practice → closing line as professor. Plus a 2-minute check every 10 minutes (one photo to judge, one term to define back, or one vote) — no lecture stretch runs longer than 10 minutes without practice.

Guards — never teach these (whole schedule)

  • Any EPCS client name or identifier, any real work-order number, address, amount, crew name, or payment handle. None appear in this schedule. (Safeguard appears only as a historical industry example in Session 4, not as an EPCS client.)
  • Any client rule as universal — the governing rulebook is the one on the order.
  • Jordan as a national ban (Washington law only). The $100 → $55 funnel as fact (fictional). The $5,000 figure as all-inclusive or current (2016 figure — re-check). Filings as work orders. Combined inventory as REO alone. Any vendor forecast as fact. Any unverified claim as fact.
  • Job photos from real work. Teaching photos are public photographs of the work type, labelled as such.
