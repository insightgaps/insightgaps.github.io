Topic 12 - Grass + Secure

About 6 minutes | Page 12 of 20 | All 20 topics

Grass and secure are the two jobs that make a vacant house look lived-in and stay closed. One is about what the street sees; the other is about who can get in.

Three things to take away

  1. First: Grass is the house first impression. An overgrown lawn tells the whole street that the house is empty. Cut to the height the order states, clear the edges, and photograph the lawn from the same place before and after, so the change can be seen.
  2. Second: Secure is about entry points, not about force. Walk every door and window, lock what can be locked, and board only where the order says to board. A locked door beside a broken window is not secure.
  3. Third: Both jobs are proved by paired photographs: same place, before and after. If the photographs do not match in place and angle, they do not prove anything — take them again, from the same spot.
