Topic 17 - Proof

About 6 minutes | Page 17 of 20 | All 20 topics

Every photograph in preservation must pass the Proof Test — four questions that decide whether a photograph is proof, or just a picture. Place, time, scope, clarity.

Three things to take away

  1. First: PLACE: does the photograph show where? A stranger must be able to tell which house it is, and which part of the house, from the photograph itself — not from the caption.
  2. Second: TIME: does the photograph show when? A date-stamp and a labelled before, during and after set are how the record proves the work happened when it says it happened.
  3. Third: SCOPE and CLARITY: does the photograph show how much — the whole job, not one corner of it — and can a stranger read it? A photograph that fails any one of the four questions fails the test.
