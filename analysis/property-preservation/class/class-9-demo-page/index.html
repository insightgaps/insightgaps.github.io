10 Follow the Money

Revenue is earned in the field but preserved at the desk. The financial life cycle traces dollars through five rigorous checkpoints.

KEY POINT 1

The Canonical Money Ladder

A line begins as a contractor BID, conforms to client ALLOWABLE, secures an APPROVED amount, submits an INVOICED sum, and settles as PAID.

KEY POINT 2

The Three Internal Desks

Vendor Manager dispatches and controls field costs; Processor verifies photos and builds invoices; Client Manager reconciles portal credits.

KEY POINT 3

The Authority Callback

Over-allowable spend requires advance written client authorization (climbing the authority ladder from Chapter 06)—never done on assumption.

Governing Rules & Compliance Standards

The Zero-Unapproved Spend Law

Work completed without prior client approval or published allowable authority will be billed at $0.00; field crews absorb unapproved work costs.

The RFO (Ready for Owner) Gate

Processors prepare invoice lines and attach photos, but only the authorized account manager or owner can apply the RFO digital signature to release submission.

Adjustment Matching Principle

Adjustments applied by client portals must be matched against original order numbers; never cross-subsidise losses across unrelated properties.

Operational Requirements & Dealing Protocols

Five-Stage Cost Ledger

Every job must track Bid, Allowable, Approved, Invoiced, and Paid figures in adjacent columns to isolate margin leakage.

Loss Code Categorization

All invoice line reductions must carry one of the 6 canonical loss codes (Proof Gap, Scope Mismatch, Timeliness, Over-Allowable, Rate Discrepancy, Portal Error).

Dealing Point — Contractor Billing Disputes

When a subcontract crew disputes a deduction, present the exact photo deficiency from the QC audit; resolve with facts, not emotion.
