Topic 16 - Damage

About 6 minutes | Page 16 of 20 | All 20 topics

Damage is what you find, not what you assume. Water, fire, weather, neglect, a break-in, plain wear — a house can be damaged in many ways, and your job is to describe what is there, not to guess how it happened.

Three things to take away

  1. First: Name what you see, not what caused it. A water mark on the ceiling is a fact. A roof that leaked is a guess. Write the fact down; leave the guess for the person who has the evidence to make it.
  2. Second: Photograph damage before you touch it. Once work starts, the before is gone. The photograph of the damage as you found it is the proof that the damage was real, and that it was there before you.
  3. Third: Some damage changes the job. If you find damage that the order did not mention, report it before you continue. Do not work around it in silence — a quiet workaround is how a small problem becomes a large one.
