Calls (FAKE, teaching only)

In the real system, calls carry transcripts, summaries, and recordings. In this demo, calls are shown as teaching summaries only: who called, about which sample order, what was decided, what is still open — no recording, no transcript, no real call. A call summary that does not name its decision and its open item is not a summary, it is a note.
