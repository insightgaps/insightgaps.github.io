Demo — Visual Twin and Duplicate Detection

DEMO — all data is fake, for class/training only, not a real work system, do not submit anything here.

Tagged photos are the real system's power source: they drive the bid and approval tracker and give a complete, detailed picture of the property, area by area, visit by visit. This demo shows that layer, which the old demo did not have at all.

Visual twin — one property, by area, across time (FAKE)

Every photo is filed by AREA (exterior front / rear / left / right, each room, each system: roof, HVAC, plumbing, electrical) and by VISIT, in Before / During / After sets. For any area of any house, the twin shows its state across time — first visit to latest — which is what bids, QC, and PCR answers are actually judged on.

Honesty bound (demo): crew photos are evidence-grade, not survey-grade. The twin is an area-mapped visual record — the powerful part for bids, QC, and submissions. Literal 3D geometry would need disciplined capture angles; this demo does not claim it.

Duplicate and reuse detection (FAKE)

The real system's differentiator: perceptual-hash duplicate / reuse detection — a capability the incumbent portal does not offer. In this demo:

Sample photo (FAKE) Hash result (FAKE, teaching) Verdict (FAKE)
Sample After view, SAMPLE-003 Hash matches no earlier photo in this sample Unique — accepted

Unflagged photos count as zero in this demo's teaching rule: a photo with no flag, no area, and no visit is not evidence, it is a file.

PCR matching and the photo gate BEFORE submission (FAKE)

PCR answers are auto-matched to the photos that prove them, and photo minimums are checked against the twin before anything is submitted. In this demo:

  • WO-EXAMPLE-003: every PCR answer in the sample has a matching photo in the twin ⇒ photo gate PASSES, order reaches owner cross-check.
  • WO-EXAMPLE-004: the cleared-area answer has no matching After photo ⇒ photo gate FAILS, order is HELD, and the price of that gate is shown as "SLA penalties avoided" in a real system — in this demo, as a teaching label, not a real dollar figure.
  • Absent from photos ≠ absent from property: a missing photo is a missing proof, never a proof of absence.

Photo QC standard (demo, from the real system): certified capture validation — timestamp vs upload time, distance from address, accuracy; required set = address / street sign, front / rear / side, roof, interior, Before / During / After, bid-support; photos, invoice, and portal must agree, or the file does not move.
