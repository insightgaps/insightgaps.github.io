Address-first rule (demo): the property is matched by ADDRESS first, never by work-order number alone. A renumbered order at the same address is the same job, not a new job plus a vanished one. That is why the Properties page lists orders under their property, and why the chain check lives here.

WO chains (FAKE, teaching only)

A submitted order can draw a follow-up order at the same property, and payment can arrive under the follow-up number. In this demo:

  • SAMPLE-001 shows WO-EXAMPLE-001 (In Field) and, as a teaching illustration, a follow-up order would be filed under SAMPLE-001, linked as "paid-under" — not as a new property.
  • No "unpaid" label on an earlier order at an address is final until the chain check runs: same property + a later order with a receipt ≈ the earlier amount ⇒ classify PAID_VIA_SUCCESSOR, with the link recorded, not unpaid.
  • Chain link types shown in this demo: supersedes / superseded-by / paid-under / adjusted-with. A superseded order leaves the ageing and loss views into an "explained unpaid" bucket.

Per-property cap ledger (FAKE, sample)
