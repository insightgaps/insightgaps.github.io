Demo — Team Access and Audit Log

DEMO — all data is fake, for class/training only, not a real work system, do not submit anything here.

In the real system, the team needs working access — not a snapshot link and not a client view. This demo shows that access model, in structure only, with no real team member in it.

Role (demo) Access in the demo story

Money rules (demo, same as the real system)

  • Money facts are sheet-only and read-only for every role. Team edits operational state only.
  • Crew-payment details are NEVER displayed in this demo, and never in a team view in a real system. This demo shows no payment handle, no payout method, and no crew-pay amount for any individual.
  • The interim team surface in the real system is the shared accounting sheet with non-amount columns only, until the full team edition is activated. In this demo, that surface is the Money page's sample ledger, labelled FAKE.
