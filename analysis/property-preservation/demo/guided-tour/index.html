Demo — Guided Tour, full flow

DEMO — all data is fake, for class/training only, not a real work system, do not submit anything here.

This tour walks the demo in the same order the real system works. The old tour had 5 stops; this one has 14, because the real system has 14 parts, not 5. Follow it in order. Nothing here submits, sends, or records anything.

Stop 1 — Property Master: one property, one record

Open Property Master — FAKE. Note the key: SAMPLE-001, keyed by ADDRESS. A renumbered order at the same address is the same job, not a new property. Chains and the cap ledger live here, not in a separate list.

Stop 2 — Intake: how work arrives

Open Intake — FAKE. Four feeds, one record: live engine photos, email instructions, payment screenshots, portal history. New-work detection: a new order at an existing property is flagged the day it appears.

Stop 3 — Live Ops: what is happening right now

Open Live Ops — FAKE. Read the table by STATE, not by order number. Count out loud: In Field 2 · Unassigned 2 · Completed — Awaiting Owner Cross-Check 2 · Held/Blocked 2. Total 8. Follow the full lifecycle: posted → assigned → scheduled → photos → QC → bid → completed → invoiced → paid → proof matched → profit known.

Stop 4 — Deadlines and Pulse: today in the field

Open Deadlines — FAKE. Escalation: silent until T-48h, digest at T-24h, direct flag at T-4h. An open follow-up is a payment-risk flag from the day it appears.

Stop 5 — Bids: price from evidence, not from habit

Open Bids — FAKE. Approval by price band, cap check, margin check before staging, Auto-Bid audit monitor. Instant approval is flagged, not celebrated — it may mean priced too low.

Stop 6 — A single order, end to end

Open Work-Order Example — FAKE. WO-EXAMPLE-003, $120 sample winterization, intake → field → photos → QC → money → cross-check. Then the contrast: WO-EXAMPLE-004, $250 sample debris removal, HELD for a proof gap. Ask: what is missing, and what is the next action? Never "submit it anyway".

Stop 7 — Photo QC, then the Visual Twin

Open Photo QC — FAKE, then Visual Twin — FAKE. QC board first: one pass, one fail on scope, one pending, judged by PLACE / TIME / SCOPE / CLARITY. Twin second: same photos by area across visits, duplicate flagged by hash, PCR matched to photos, photo gate BEFORE submission. Absent from photos ≠ absent from property.

Stop 8 — Submission Queue: the gate

Open Submission Queue — FAKE. Read the readiness checklist, then the standing rule: complete → report → owner cross-check → submit ONLY on the owner's word, one job at a time. In this demo, the sequence STOPS at owner cross-check. RFO is the owner's own step; this demo never marks it.

Stop 9 — Money, then the War Room

Open Money — FAKE, then Exceptions — FAKE. Money ladder: claimed $1,200 → approved $1,000 → invoiced $1,000 → paid $750 → outstanding $250, gross = net, all sample. War Room next: payment state machine, three leakage ledgers (never one blended loss), Income Ratio verdicts (Scale / Monitor / Reprice), adjustment queue — a recorded decision, never a quiet edit.

Stop 10 — Losses and disputes

Open Losses — FAKE. Every loss carries a mandatory reason code; UNEXPLAINED is a queue driven to zero. Chain-paid is not unpaid. A chargeback without reference + reason + evidence is disputable by construction.

Stop 11 — Properties, Clients, Crew, Quo

Open Properties — FAKE (search view), Clients — FAKE (economics, aging, portfolio decisions), Crew — FAKE (roster + dispatch), then Quo — FAKE (unanswered queue, response rate, behaviour, merged dispatch). Crew intelligence is built from data, not impressions.

Stop 12 — Growth and Team Access

Open Growth — FAKE (pipeline board, structure only — no application or outreach in this demo, gated on the owner's authorization in a real system), then Team Access — FAKE (roles, money read-only, audit log, crew-payment details never displayed).

Stop 13 — Command Board

Open Command Board — FAKE. Today's deadlines, digest in Question → Finding → Impact → Action form, parity (sample), sync health, watches silent unless something changed. If a number cannot be traced to a demo table, it does not belong in a brief.

Stop 14 — Meeting Brief and Fake Seed

Open Meeting Brief — FAKE, then Fake seed — FAKE. The brief is computed from the demo tables; the seed is the full fake dataset behind it, in one place. Teach numbers only from the demo pages, never as real figures.
