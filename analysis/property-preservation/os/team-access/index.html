Audit log - structure

The snapshot was built from daily batches dated 2026-01-01 through 2026-11-10 (see the snapshot manifest). The audit log is the sequence of those daily batches: 3131 days of batches, frozen as of 2026-11-10. After 2026-12-31 the feed ends and the snapshot stays frozen at 2026-11-10.
