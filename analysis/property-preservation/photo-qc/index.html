Photo QC and datestamps

Photo sets are checked before they are submitted: before, during, and after sets are separated, each photo is flagged to its set, and datestamps follow the receiving system's rule. Some systems require a date stamp on photos; others do not. The rule is per-system, and a photo that is only datestamped in its file metadata is not the same as a photo with a visible date stamp where one is required.

QC discipline in the sample workflow: flag one photo set at a time, verify the flagged filename in the tooltip or filename check, work in pilot batches, and never mark a job ready for the office until its photo QC is complete. A failed pressure test, a stuck valve, or any other defect visible in a photo is a work decision, not a photo problem to be flagged away.
