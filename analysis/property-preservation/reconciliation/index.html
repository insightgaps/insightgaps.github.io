Reconciliation and loss-reason discipline

Reconciliation is not a single total. It is a set of separate questions, each answered per property, per work type, and per crew: what was invoiced, what was received, what was paid to the crew, what is genuinely unpaid, what was booked as a loss, and what was received with no crew cost recorded. A received-but-unexpended balance at one property is first checked against that property's losses before it is treated as free money.

Every loss carries a mandatory reason code. UNEXPLAINED is not a reason — it is a queue, driven to zero. A loss booked entirely as a loss, with no client deduction verified, is an unverified deduction until it is verified or disputed with evidence, not a settled write-off.
