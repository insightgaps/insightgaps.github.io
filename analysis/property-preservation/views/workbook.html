Core Spreadsheet Calculation Formulas
Margin Overrun %
=IFERROR((Expected_Cost - Actual_Paid) / Expected_Cost, 0)

Calculates overruns by comparing expecting payouts against actual payments made to identify leaks.

Receivables Aging
=IF(Paid_Status="Paid", 0, TODAY() - Invoice_Date)

Tracks how long invoices remain outstanding, sorting them into 61–90 and 90+ days aging columns.

Income Multiplier
=SUMIFS(Received_Amt, Client, A2) / SUMIFS(Contractor_Cost, Client, A2)

Divides cash collections by expenses to evaluate overall client account profitability.
