Executive Summary
Question

Which service lines generate positive operating margins, and which destroy value?

Finding

Initial Services yields the highest returns (1.33× ratio), while high-volume Grass Recuts (0.83×) operate at an operational deficit.

Impact

High-volume deficit lines generate a combined $6.9K loss, directly consuming margins generated by profitable service lines.

Action

Immediately reprice or halt Grass Recuts and Evictions, scale Initial Services, and protect the profitable service margins.

Finding 01

Highest Leakage Services: Initial secure work suffers from extreme validation friction.

Evidence

Follow Up carries a 25.2% leakage rate (1 work order), followed by SNOW REMOVAL at 7.3% (23 work orders) and Bid Approval at 7.1% (97 work orders).

Operational Implication

Despite high headline margins, initial property secures suffer from heavy client-side rejection due to strict photo validation guidelines. Compliance checks must be reinforced at the field dispatch level.

Finding 02

Value Destroyers: Five core service types operate below the danger threshold.

Evidence

Initial Services (1.33×), SNOW REMOVAL (1.23×), and Allowable Approvals (1.16×) generate healthy margins. Conversely, Eviction (0.48×), Grass Cut (0.51×), and Grass Recuts (0.83×) represent absolute operational deficits.

Operational Implication

A ratio below 1.0× means the business pays contractors more than clients reimburse, ignoring company overhead. Continuing these services without renegotiating rates acts as a direct capital drain.

Finding 03

Strategic Imbalance: The highest-volume service line generates systemic losses.

Operational Implication

Operational focus is inverted: high-volume services are destroying cash. Volume scale must be restricted in Grass Recuts and Bid Approvals while pushing resources into Allowable Approvals and Winterizations.

Service Portfolio Decisions
  • Scale

    Allowable Approval & Winterization: Scale these categories immediately. They generate positive working capital and drive overall business profitability.

  • Monitor

    Initial Secure, Bid Request: Monitor closely. These service lines are profitable but carry moderate leakage rates due to photo documentation validation friction.

  • Reprice

    Grass Recut, Maid Service, Bid Approval, Eviction: Enforce rate increases on clients or renegotiate contractor fees. If pricing cannot be adjusted, stop performing these services.

Methodology

Dataset: Based on 480 work order records spanning November 2025 to June 2026. Data source includes invoicing ledgers and client validation portals.

Calculation Logic: Income Ratio represents (Client Payments Received) ÷ (Contractor Cost). Leakage Rate represents (Total Leakage) ÷ (Invoiced Amount).

Limitations: Calculations are volume-weighted and assume fixed operational overhead holds constant across all order categories.
