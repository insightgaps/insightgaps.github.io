Core Logic

Spreadsheet Calculation Mappings

The workbook uses standardized logic blocks to audit invoices and isolate underperforming assets. These formulas form the backbone of the operational auditing system:

Margin Erosion %

=IFERROR((Expected_Cost - Actual_Paid) / Expected_Cost, 0)

Measures direct margin loss by comparing the contractor payout terms against the actual payments issued, helping spot cost overruns.

Receivables Aging

=IF(Paid_Status="Paid", 0, TODAY() - Invoice_Date)

Calculates the duration cash remains uncollected since the invoice date, automatically routing days past due into 61–90 and 90+ buckets.

Income Efficiency

=SUMIFS(Received_Amt, Client, A2) / SUMIFS(Contractor_Cost, Client, A2)

Ranks clients by their capital multiplier, dividing total cash collected by contractor expenses incurred to ensure a ratio above 1.0×.

Methodology & Tool Setup

Data Connection: The Looker Studio dashboard relies on the Google Sheets workbook as a live data source. By adding new work orders to the bottom of the master sheet, all KPI cards and charts refresh instantly.

Anonymization: All addresses, contractor names, and pricing structures have been masked to preserve business confidentiality while maintaining the exact proportional margins.
