Submission-queue discipline

The submission sequence is fixed, and it is a sequence, not a single click:

  1. Complete the work.
  2. Report the finished state.
  3. The owner cross-checks it himself.
  4. Submit only on the owner's word, after that cross-check, one job at a time.

Never submit on a green-light judgment alone. Never mark a job ready for the office unless its tracking is complete and its photo QC is complete — and some jobs have a standing rule to track and complete everything but not mark ready for the office, which the queue must respect, not override.
