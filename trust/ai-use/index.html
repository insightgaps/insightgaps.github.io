AI Use Disclosure

  • AI is used to audit: flag claims without a source, check arithmetic, and surface outliers.
  • AI is not used to originate a claim, a number, a causal explanation, a headline that overstates a finding, or a synthetic image.
  • A human editor is responsible for every published word and number, as if it were written by a human.
  • Unpublished notes, source identities, and embargoed findings are never put into a hosted AI tool.
  • Where AI materially assisted a specific report, that report's evidence panel says so.

Insight Gaps Bureau — Independent forensic data journalism from Dhaka, Bangladesh. Evidence before narrative. Every claim traceable to a source, or marked untraced.
