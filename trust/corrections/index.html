Corrections

This is the permanent corrections log. Every correction here also appears dated on the affected story itself. A log entry nobody can find from the story is not a correction system. This log is append-only: entries are never edited or deleted; new entries are appended at the end.

  • C-001 — 2026-06-08 — Blood Routes: The report's night-time crash statistic was published as 97.13% (derived from a dataset later found to contain about 95% synthetic padding); it was corrected to 41.56% (RSF Dhaka Annual Report 2025 basis) on 2026-06-08, after the pipeline retracted the original figure on 2026-06-03. Amended: night-time share of fatal Dhaka crashes corrected 97.13% → 41.56% (10pm–6am window).
  • C-002 — 2026-09-03 — Blood Routes: The Eid-ul-Adha 2025 timeline entry described a "29% year-on-year surge" in road fatalities. The report's own timeline data (312 deaths in 2025 vs 262 in 2024) yields a 19.08% increase, not 29%. Amended: timeline description corrected to "Sharp 19% year-on-year rise (312 vs 262 in 2024)".
  • C-003 — 2026-09-03 — The Impunity Machine: A note disclosing that the Bangladesh 0.46% conviction rate and the UK, India, and South Africa comparator rates use different denominators (all complaints registered at One-Stop Crisis Centres versus prosecuted trials in court) was added to the comparison section after initial publication. Amended: comparison chart now carries the denominator note; the comparison must not be read as like-for-like conviction rates.
  • C-004 — 2026-09-03 — The Impunity Machine: The published source count of 91 named sources overstated the archived source registry, which contains 65 entries (54 CONFIRMED, 11 PROBABLE). 27 IDs in the S-01 to S-91 sequence had no registry entries. Amended: source count corrected to 65 across the report, detailed report, methodology page, manifest, and evidence page; S-26 re-mapped to registry entry S-37 (UNFPA/BBS VAW Survey 2024 Highlights); S-27 and S-28 parenthetical citations removed, source names retained without dead IDs.
  • C-005 — 2026-09-03 — The Lead Belt: Several published figures were internally inconsistent with the report's own embedded dataset: the "Satellite Active" filter chip said 26 while the data flags 19 high-confidence sites; the critical-areas label did not distinguish 68 critical sites from the 65 exceeding 100,000 ppm; the headline stated "507 upazila boundaries" while the embedded map payload contains 93 upazila polygons; the choropleth's student model (400 students per school) summed to 58,000 while the headline used 275 per school (39,875); and the 39,875 figure was described as "children at extreme risk" rather than as a projection of exposure instances. The manifest publication date (2025-01-01) also contradicted repository history (first published 2026-05-25). Amended: labels reconciled ("Satellite confirmed: 26 (high-confidence: 19)"; "Critical: 68 · 65 exceed 100,000 ppm"; upazila count 93; choropleth regenerated at ×275, summing to 39,875; headline reframed as about 39,875 exposure instances, unique-school estimate about 33,275, audited range 24,650–50,750); the 59 m closest-school claim scoped to the featured site; provenance note added documenting the snapshot-replication discrepancy (166 / 51 / 125 versus 145 / 44 / 121); manifest date corrected to 2026-05-25.

No other corrections are recorded in the live corrections log at the time of this migration. If a correction is found later, it will be added here dated, stating what changed, as well as on the affected story.
