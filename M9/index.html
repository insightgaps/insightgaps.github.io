Money Leaks at Every Handoff: Follow the Dollar

"It is 4:45 PM on a hot Friday. Marcus, your best field contractor, drops a handwritten $1,000 invoice onto Elena's desk at the preservation office. He spent nine hours in ninety-degree heat clearing an overgrown jungle, securing broken entry doors, and hauling rotted debris at Maple House. Marcus sees $1,000 of honest sweat in the sun. But Elena sits at the Processor desk facing the national client's audit portal. She knows that what the back earns in the field must survive four administrative choke points before the bank wire clears. Marcus billed $1,000; the bank will remit $550. Where did the missing $450 go? The client didn't steal it—it bled out inside our own handoffs."

KEY POINT 1 • THE FIVE RUNGS & BLEED OWNERSHIP

The Field Earns the Bid; The Office Earns the Cash

BID → ALLOWABLE → APPROVED → INVOICED → PAID. Never start from "Claimed." Work performed in the dirt is not money in the bank. Every handoff has a leak, and every leak has an owner: Processor owns the $200 allowability leak; Vendor Manager owns the $100 unapproved over-allowable drop; Client Manager owns the $150 deduction and adjustment gap. A loss without an owner is money lost again next Friday.

KEY POINT 2 • BID RULES & THE 5-DAY GATE

No Scope, No Rate, No Evidence = No Bid

Every valid bid requires three pillars: Scope (Order) + Rate (Rulebook) + Evidence (Field). If damages exceed the allowable schedule, submit an Over-Allowable Bid at least 5 business days before conveyance. Hard rule: work started before written portal approval is work donated to the bank. Remember the preservation standard: gross equals net—no commercial retail discounts.

KEY POINT 3 • HONEST AUDIT & ADDRESS MATCHING

Measure the Pile; Follow the Address

Marcus guessed 15 cubic yards from his truck bed; geometry proved 9×6×3 ft = 162 cu ft ÷ 27 = 6.0 CY. Fix the invoice at the Allowable rung—strike unallowable TV hauling, correct inflated volumes, and hold unproven locks for field re-trip photos. When WO-A shows $0 paid, search the physical street address to find WO-B ($420 CHAIN-PAID). Remember: UNEXPLAINED is an active emergency queue driven to $0 weekly, never a resting place.
