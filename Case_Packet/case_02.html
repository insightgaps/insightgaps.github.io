When the Economy Loop Breaks, the Family Leaves Long Before the Wood Decays.

"November 2008. David Miller sat at the kitchen table beneath the fluorescent ring light, staring at a stack of final notices. When the local manufacturing plant cut shifts and his adjustable rate mortgage reset, the monthly payment jumped by $640. He applied for loan modification; he waited out the 120-day federal floor. But when permanent layoffs hit, David and his wife packed their two children and what clothes would fit into a rented trailer on a freezing Friday night. David pulled the door shut and whispered, 'I'm sorry.' The house did not break. The macroeconomic loop broke."
Asset CASE_02: The circular macroeconomic feedback loop, the 5-stage foreclosure road, and the vacancy decay cascade.
Three Sticky Anchors to Remember
  1. 1
    The Loop Snaps First: Jobs, interest rates, and credit standards drive home prices; when payment shock hits, default happens long before the physical wood rots.
  2. 2
    Help Before Foreclosure: Federal CFPB rules enforce an invariant 120-day loss mitigation floor (forbearance, modification, short sale) before legal foreclosure can begin.
  3. 3
    Three Separate States: Never confuse Property State (empty), Legal State (pre-foreclosure), and Work Order State (unauthorized)—each is governed by separate rules.
