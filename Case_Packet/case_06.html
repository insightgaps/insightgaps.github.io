Case 6 of 7 • Retelling Stop: Money Module M9–M10 • The Money Staircase

Every Lost Dollar Has an Owner: The Forensic Bleed from BID to Remittance.

"Friday, 4:45 PM. Sarah and Elena sat side by side before dual monitors. On the left screen, the contractor billed $1,000 for work at Maple House. On the right, the client remittance check arriving 60 days later showed exactly $550. Elena asked, 'Where did $450 go?' Sarah pointed to the audit logs: '$200 unallowable debris and missing photos at my desk; $100 unapproved repair at dispatch; and $150 split into $60 short-pay + $50 disputed quantity + $40 property adjustment. Every lost dollar has a desk owner. Nothing disappeared into thin air.'"
Asset CASE_06: The canonical money staircase starting from BID, role bleed attribution, and 6 unified loss codes.
Three Sticky Anchors to Remember
  1. 1
    Staircase Starts from BID: Canonical sequence: BID ($1,000) → ALLOWABLE ($800) → APPROVED ($700) → INVOICED ($700) → PAID ($550).
  2. 2
    Bleed Ownership Across Three Desks: Leaks belong to the Processor (unallowable scope), Vendor Manager (unapproved work), or Client Manager (unappealed rejections).
  3. 3
    Chain Detective Matches by Address: An order closed at $0 followed by payment under another order at the same address is CHAIN-PAID, not delinquent debt.
