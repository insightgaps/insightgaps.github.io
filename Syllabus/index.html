The Honest Certification Boundary

This five-hour program delivers industry literacy and audited classroom decision skills. It does not confer autonomous field authority or legal certification. Three distinct statuses exist and are never blended:

✓ 1. Attended

Granted upon complete attendance across all 300 minutes. Verifies exposure to industry terminology, the 5-link chain, and the 11-stop lifecycle.

★ 2. Demonstrated in Class

Earned exclusively by passing the Cold Capstone Exam (Module 10) against the objective 4-trap detection rubric and 25-word Update Line standard.

⚑ 3. Operationally Authorised

NOT granted by classroom attendance alone. Conferred solely by employer onboarding, client credentialing, background clearance, and live supervision.
