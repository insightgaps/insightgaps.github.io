Syllabus

Fourteen chapters that follow one house — from the year she was built to the day she is a home again.

Class The Story Course 14 chapters
  1. 01 The Rise of the American House How America got its houses — the boom, the loans, and the filter.
  2. 02 The Peaks and the Loop Two building waves, and the money loop that fed them.
  3. 03 Who Looks After an Empty House The chain of hands, the Safeguard example, and the 2008 flood.
  4. 04 How a House Falls From a missed payment to an empty house, step by step.
  5. 05 Proof, Rules, and Many Clients Three eras of proof, one courtroom question, and a rulebook per client.
  6. 06 One Work Order, Eleven Stops A single order's whole journey, from intake to follow-up.
  7. 07 The First Jobs: Grass and Doors The face of the house, and every opening that has to be closed.
  8. 08 Winter, Debris, and Inspection Three more jobs, and the proof each one demands.
  9. 09 How to Read a Damaged House Water, mold, weather, and neglect — and what to do first.
  10. 10 Follow the Money A bid, five leaks, and the three desks that move the money.
  11. 11 The Last House, With No Help Loose money, an unseen house, and a verdict defended in twenty-five words.
  12. 12 2008: The Flood The year the system broke — and the industry it left behind.
  13. 13 One Job, Eight Stations The work as a circle — and the gaps where jobs die.
  14. 14 The Work Itself All five jobs in one pass, on one house.
