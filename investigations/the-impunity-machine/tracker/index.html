The Impunity Machine — Tracker

This page preserves the old URL /investigations/the-impunity-machine/tracker/. In the new structure, it is an aggregate tracker only. The live case-level tracker, which used case records and automated leads, is not republished here, because those records are held pending source-protection review and automated leads start as UNVERIFIED. A tracker that cannot be updated on a stated schedule, and cannot be verified in aggregate, is worse than no tracker.

On this page: In short · What we do not know · Aggregate tracker totals · Method · Evidence · Right of reply · Corrections · What to watch next

In short

  • Pending cases: about 150,000 (March 2025).
  • 2024 queue growth: 11,896 (17,544 filings minus 5,648 disposals).
  • DNA unprocessed: 1,204 of 1,304 (92%), September 2025.
  • Average verdict wait: 2,349 days; 50 of 64 districts with no full One-Stop Crisis Centre.

What we do not know

  • No live, automatically updated case count is published on this page. The figures below are the published totals from the main report, frozen at their publication dates, not a live feed.
  • If a figure moves, it will be updated here dated, with its source, or it will stay as a stated gap.

Method and limitations

Totals are taken from the main report and its methodology page, with the same denominator discipline: conviction rates are never averaged across denominators, and the CEDAW 53.52% disposal rate is never shown as a conviction rate. No new total is created on this page.
