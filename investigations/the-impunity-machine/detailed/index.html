The Impunity Machine — Detailed report

This page preserves the old URL /investigations/the-impunity-machine/detailed/. In the new structure, the full report lives at The Impunity Machine. This page is a consolidated detailed summary, with aggregate data only. No case-level records, no identifying detail, and no case tracker data are republished here — that is a deliberate safety decision, not an oversight, because the live case-level files are held pending source-protection review.

On this page: In short · What we do not know · Method · Evidence · Right of reply · Corrections · What to watch next

In short

  • Conviction-rate range: 0.12% to 3.66% on different denominators — never averaged.
  • DNA backlog: 92%, 1,204 of 1,304 samples in 2025.
  • Average verdict wait: 2,349 days.
  • Confirmed-absent datasets: twelve, GA-001 to GA-012, listed in the main report and its methodology page.
  • Probability model: floor 0.00034%, mid-reference 0.0215%, ceiling 0.2756%.

What we do not know

  • Case timelines and the monthly ledger are not reproduced here. They are live-report detail held pending source-protection review.
  • Any figure that cannot be stated in aggregate is left as a gap, not filled from case records.
Method and limitations

Method is the main report's method: source hierarchy from government statistics down to international assessments, evidence tiers CONFIRMED / PROBABLE / CONFIRMED ABSENT, denominator discipline for every conviction rate, and the probability model with its sensitivity range (floor 0.00034%, mid-reference 0.0215%, ceiling 0.2756%). See the methodology page for the full method, including the conviction-rate table and the three probability-model baselines.

Evidence panel

Claim register: claims/BD-INV-002.json. Dataset: data/BD-INV-002.csv (aggregate only). The public digest of the master evidence file is referenced in the main report; the full master workbook is private-held pending source-protection review.

Right of reply

As in the main report: no reply dates are invented here. A fresh, logged reply attempt is required before any new allegation beyond what is already published.

Corrections on this page

What to watch next
