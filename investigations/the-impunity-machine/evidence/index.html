The Impunity Machine — Evidence appendix

This appendix holds the full detail that is not in the main visual scroll. Nothing here is new: it is the method, evidence panel, right-of-reply table, and corrections from the migrated report, preserved in full. Aggregate only — no case-level records.

Method and limitations

Sources: 65 named sources in the archived registry (54 CONFIRMED, 11 PROBABLE in live-report tier language), including Police Headquarters crime statistics, Supreme Court annual reports, National Forensic DNA Profiling Laboratory reports, CEDAW Committee document CEDAW/C/BGD/CO/8, Government of Bangladesh submissions to the UN Secretary-General (May 2024), UNFPA/BBS Violence Against Women Survey 2024, BRAC 2022, BRAC/Agile, Naripokkho, BLAST, UN Women, and named investigative journalism citing primary documents (Daily Star, Prothom Alo, TBS News).

Source hierarchy, as published: (1) government official statistics, (2) UN treaty body documents, (3) peer-reviewed studies with primary data collection, (4) major NGO programme evaluations, (5) named investigative journalism citing primary documents, (6) named journalism for individual case timeline verification only, (7) international assessments citing primary data.

Tier system, as published in the live methodology: CONFIRMED means established with high confidence from primary or official sources (two or more independent primary sources, or one official source plus corroboration, or a court record, UN treaty document, or directly cited official statistic). PROBABLE means likely accurate but below the CONFIRMED threshold (one named primary source plus a corroborating pattern, or a calculation derived from two or more CONFIRMED inputs where the derivation is stated). CONFIRMED ABSENT means a data point that should exist but demonstrably does not, after active search across three or more institutional channels. This migrated report maps CONFIRMED to Confirmed, PROBABLE to Supported, and CONFIRMED ABSENT to a gap entry in "What we do not know" (with the absence itself graded Confirmed where the search is documented). The live methodology states it does not use an ALLEGED tier for any primary claim, and no claim appears without a named source.

Twelve confirmed-absent datasets (GA-001 to GA-012, as grouped in the main report, Step 7): annual conviction rates by tribunal and district; FIR registration rates; 90-day compliance rates; national §17 prosecution counts; withdrawal and abandonment rates; pre/post-2020 conviction comparisons; DNA processing times by district and case type; the witness protection law (drafted, pending since 2015, not enacted); the marital rape exemption (CEDAW required removal in 2016, still in force in 2026); district-level conviction data; disposal-versus-conviction breakdowns; §32 hospital-refusal tracking.

Limitations: conviction-rate denominators differ and must never be averaged; the probability model assumes independence between funnel stages, which is a simplification; survey inputs carry confidence intervals and self-report biases; the live report's automated live-record collector (GitHub Actions, every six hours, RSS feeds classified by an AI tool, deduplicated against existing leads) assigns UNVERIFIED to all automated items, upgraded only after manual cross-reference — no automated item reaches the narrative without human verification, and this migrated report does not republish any automated lead or case record.

Public data only. No RTI, interview, or fieldwork is used in this migrated report. The live report's gaps that would require unpublished government data are stated as gaps, not filled.
