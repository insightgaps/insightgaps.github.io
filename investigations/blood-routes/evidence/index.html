Blood Routes — Evidence Appendix

This technical appendix records the methodology, audited data pipelines, published official record, and corrections log for the Blood Routes investigation.

Methodology and Data Cleaning Audit

Primary Sources: World Health Organization (WHO) Global Status Report on Road Safety 2023; Bangladesh Road Transport Authority (BRTA) Annual Report 2023; Road Safety Foundation (RSF) Annual and Eid monitoring releases (2022–2026); Bangladesh Jatri Kalyan Samity (BJKS) transport monitoring reports; Transparency International Bangladesh (TIB) Private Bus Transport Sector Study 2024; World Bank "Delivering Road Safety in Bangladesh" 2022; and the Figshare academic repository dataset "Bangladesh Road Traffic Accident Dataset (2007–2024): Multi-Source Integration" (DOI: 10.6084/m9.figshare.30631292.v1; 47,680 raw rows × 31 columns).

Data Sanitisation & Deduplication Audit:

  • The raw academic repository contains 47,680 rows. Forensic inspection revealed that 95.11% of the entries were artificial duplicates created by cross-joining single collision events across disparate victim sub-tables.
  • Rows were grouped across nine identifying collision coordinates: location string, parsed event date, time, latitude, longitude, driver age, narrative text, vehicle type, and casualty count.
  • Fatalities and injuries were consolidated to the verified maximum per unique event. Deduplication yielded exactly 2,331 unique accidents (4.89% of the raw repository rows).
  • Raw 'Year' and 'Month' columns were found to be synthetic padding; true event timestamps were extracted directly from the narrative date strings, isolating the verified series to 2022–2024 (2022: 334 crashes; 2023: 4 crashes [a documented data collection anomaly]; 2024: 1,993 crashes).
  • 143 records contained the corrupted string IMG 2467 in the date column, proving unvalidated optical/camera file metadata was injected into the source registry.

Definitional Reconciliation:

  • BRTA Official Count: 5,480 fatalities (2023). Counts only victims certified dead at the scene in police First Information Reports (FIRs). Excludes victims succumbing en route or in hospital, and collisions settled out-of-court.
  • WHO Epidemiological Model: ~31,578 fatalities (2023). Based on the standardised 30-day post-collision mortality definition.
  • Ratio: 31,578 / 5,480 = 5.76× (reported as 5.8× in text). The discrepancy reflects institutional counting criteria, not sampling error.
