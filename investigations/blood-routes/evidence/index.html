Blood Routes — Evidence appendix

This appendix holds the full detail that is not in the main visual scroll. Nothing here is new: it is the method, evidence panel, right-of-reply table, and corrections from the migrated report, preserved in full.

Method and limitations

Sources: WHO modelled estimates, BRTA official counts, Road Safety Foundation and Bangladesh Jatri Kalyan Samity annual and Eid monitoring reports, Transparency International Bangladesh 2024 transport survey, World Bank 2022 vehicle-rate comparison (102 deaths per 10,000 registered vehicles, 19.3 per 100,000 population, WHO 2023), and the Figshare academic dataset "Bangladesh Road Traffic Accident Dataset (2007–2024): Multi-Source Integration" (DOI 10.6084/m9.figshare.30631292.v1), 47,680 rows × 31 columns.

Cleaning steps: rows grouped by nine identifying fields (location, parsed date, time, latitude, longitude, driver age, accident narrative, vehicle info, victim category); fatality and injury counts aggregated by maximum per incident; result 2,331 unique accidents (4.89% of raw rows). The raw Year and Month columns were discarded as mismatched padding; true dates were parsed from date strings, giving 2022–2024 (2022: 334, 2023: 4, 2024: 1,993 unique crashes in the audit text — note the 2023 count of 4 is itself a data-quality warning, not a real annual crash count). 143 rows contained the string "IMG 2467" instead of a valid date, indicating camera metadata mistakenly integrated into the date column.

Definitions and sensitivity: BRTA counts police-FIR spot-recorded deaths; WHO uses a modelled 30-day definition. Changing the definition changes the total by a factor of about 5.8, which is the finding — not a rounding difference. Dhaka map points are exact source coordinates; non-Dhaka points are visual corridor placements, not street-level claims, per the live report's map provenance note.

Public data only. No RTI, interview, or fieldwork is used in this migrated report. The live report mentions an RTI application as possible for the beneficial-owner registry; this programme does not pursue RTI, so that route is stated as a gap, not a method step here.
