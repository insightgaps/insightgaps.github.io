Contact

To submit a tip, report an error, or ask about a dataset, use the bureau contact address published in the live site's site manifest: insightgaps@gmail.com. Do not send sensitive personal data, unpublished documents, or identifying information about vulnerable people through an unencrypted channel.

Corrections: if you believe a published finding is wrong, tell us the claim, the source, and what you believe is correct. Corrections are shown dated on the affected story as well as in the corrections log.

No personal name, photo, workplace, or personal account is published on this site. Correspondence about a specific investigation should cite its investigation ID (for example BD-INV-001) and the claim ID from its claim register, so the correction can be traced to the exact claim.

Insight Gaps Bureau — Independent forensic data journalism from Dhaka, Bangladesh. Evidence before narrative. Every claim traceable to a source, or marked untraced.
