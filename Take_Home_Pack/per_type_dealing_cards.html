Per-Type Dealing Cards: Typical Scope — Always Check the Order

"When a work order drops into your queue, you don't guess what the national client allows—you read the ticket. Each service has a typical boundary, an evidence threshold, and a failure trap that costs money. Master the cards before you touch the house."

KEY POINT 1 • EXTERIOR PROTOCOLS

Recut Diaries, Key Codes, and Fences

Track lawn recuts on a 10–14 day cycle in the office recut diary. Exterior locks must be rekeyed to the exact client key code with lockbox tumblers scrambled. Never assume vacancy: in Washington State, Jordan v. Nationstar makes pre-foreclosure lockouts illegal under RCW 7.28.230.

KEY POINT 2 • INTERIOR DISCIPLINES

Pink Antifreeze, Honest Yards, and Property Rights

Pink propylene glycol antifreeze must be clearly visible in all toilet bowls, tanks, and sink traps. Measure debris by geometry (L×W×H÷27); never touch personal property of value without a formal court order. Broom-sweep all floors from the corners out.

KEY POINT 3 • FORENSIC DAMAGE TRIAD

Safety First, Proof Second, Work Third

Read the property from the sky down. Classify damage across five kinds: Water (find source first), Mold (never paint over active mold), Vandalism (secure envelope first), Weather (tarp roof), Neglect (differentiate aging from insurable events). An inspection fixes nothing—it tells the truth about everything.
