KEY POINT 1 • THE CANONICAL MONEY STAIRCASE

From BID to PAID: Every Drop Has an Owner

Money drops through 5 rungs: BID ($1,000) → ALLOWABLE ($800) [-$200 leak owned by Processor: $120 unallowable + $80 unproven] → APPROVED ($700) [-$100 leak owned by Vendor Manager: unapproved scope] → INVOICED ($700) [gross = net] → PAID ($550) [-$150 leak owned by Client Manager & Processor: $60 rejections + $50 short-pays + $40 property adjustment].

KEY POINT 2 • LOSS RECONCILIATION & ADJUSTMENTS

Six Loss Codes; Unexplained Driven to Zero

Every deduction must be tagged: REJECTED (cure proof), SHORT-PAID (audit CY math), UNALLOWABLE (vendor cost), ADJUSTED (surplus offset), CHAIN-PAID (match address), or UNEXPLAINED (active audit queue, driven to zero weekly). Canonical Adjustment Math: $500 client surplus − $300 prior verified loss = $200 unspent reserve on property ledger, signed by Authorised Approver.
