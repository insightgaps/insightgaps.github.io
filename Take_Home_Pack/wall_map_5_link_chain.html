WALL MAP • THE CUSTODIAL SPINE

The 11-Stop Order Lifecycle: Pass It Clean or Stop It Honestly

"One instruction leaves a lender's high-rise in Dallas, crosses a servicer's default desk in Ohio, passes into a national dispatch portal, lands in our office engine, and sends Marcus down a gravel driveway in Pennsylvania. Eleven stops. If the evidence breaks at Stop 7, the order doesn't limp forward—it travels backward to the field."

KEY POINT 1 • THE FIVE-LINK CHAIN

Five Separate Desks Guard One House

Bank → Servicer → National → Office → Crew. The bank sets allowable ceilings; the servicer enforces the 120-day pre-foreclosure loss mitigation floor; the national manages 3,143 counties; the office proves the work; the crew turns the deadbolt. No single link sees what the whole chain sees.

KEY POINT 2 • STOP 3: THE PERMISSION GATE

Authority First, Tools Second

Before a crew cuts a lock or boards a window, they must verify legal authority. In Washington State, Jordan v. Nationstar bars pre-foreclosure lockouts as unlawful possession under lien theory. If a refrigerator hums or a clock is lit, declare HOLD & ESCALATE. Uncertainty is never resolved by guessing.

KEY POINT 3 • STOP 7: THE REJECTION LOOP

Deficiencies Go Back; Never Papered Over

At Stop 7, the Processor audits the photo roll across PLACE / TIME / SCOPE / CLARITY. A missing rear yard shot or unreadable pressure gauge triggers an immediate return trip to the field. The Processor completes the file to Ready; only the Authorised Approver marks RFO.
