Governing Rules & Compliance Standards

The 35 PSI Pressure Mandate

Plumbing lines must maintain 35 PSI of dry air for 30 minutes with zero pressure drop; line breach voids charge.

Cubic Yard Calculation Standard

Debris volume = (L × W × H in feet) ÷ 27; subjective visual estimates are rejected without tape measure proof.

Blight Prevention Rule

Turf must be cut under municipal threshold and clippings swept from all paved walks.

Operational Requirements & Dealing Protocols

Non-Toxic Antifreeze Proof

Photograph pink propylene glycol in all toilet bowls, traps, and dishwashers.

Dealing Point — Emergency Leaks

If active plumbing leaks are found upon arrival, shut main street stop immediately and alert emergency dispatch.
