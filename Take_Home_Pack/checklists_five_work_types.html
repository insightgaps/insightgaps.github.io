KEY POINT 1 • EXTERIOR CRAFT

Grass Is the Face; Secure Is the Permission Slip

Grass Cut & Recut: Full lot coverage required (front, sides, rear, curb swale). Missing the wide rear after shot disallows the entire invoice. Initial Secure: Replace exterior deadbolts to client code; mount lockbox with tumblers spun off; board broken entries with carriage bolts. Remember: Wrongful lockout triggers catastrophic civil trespass liability.

KEY POINT 2 • WINTERIZATION INTEGRITY

Never Fake a Failed Pressure Test

Shut water meter, drain water heater (breaker OFF first!), blow supply lines clear with air, and pour pink non-toxic propylene glycol antifreeze into all toilet bowls, tanks, and sink traps. Hold 35–40 PSI static test: if the needle drops, NEVER mark pass. Declare HOLD / SYSTEM FAILED PRESSURE TEST, photograph the drop, and bid repair.

KEY POINT 3 • HONEST MATH & INSPECTION TRUTH

Measure the Pile; Report the Condition

Debris CY Formula: $\mathbf{L \times W \times H \div 27}$. A $9'\times 6'\times 3'$ pile equals exactly 6 CY; billing 15 CY is fraud. Never touch personal property of value without a formal court order. Property Inspection: Inspector fixes nothing—the written survey and photo roll IS the product.
