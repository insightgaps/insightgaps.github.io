KEY POINT 1 • THE WHOLE CHAIN OR NO MONEY

Five Desks, Eleven Stops, One Clean Handoff

A work order travels from Bank → Servicer → National → Office → Crew before a deadbolt turns. The field earns the bid, but the office earns the cash. An order incomplete in proof or missing authority never clears the final rung. Remember the standing law: Vendor Manager gets the right crew to the right house with permission → Processor turns field proof into a clean, honest invoice → Client Manager turns the invoice into cash and explains every dollar that did not come back.

KEY POINT 2 • INVARIANT CORE TRUTHS

Permission First, Proof Always, Math Honest

FHA insures loans—it never lends money. Jordan v. Nationstar applies to Washington State lien theory ONLY. Never fake a winterization pressure test: a dropped needle is forensic diagnostic truth, so report HOLD. Honest debris is measured by length times width times height divided by twenty-seven (L×W×H÷27). When signs of life whisper, type twenty-five words and back away to the curb.

KEY POINT 3 • THE THREE NON-BLENDED STATUSES

Attended vs Demonstrated vs Authorised

Attended means complete classroom literacy. Demonstrated in Class means independently auditing the cold capstone file with all four deliberate traps caught and authoring an approved update line. Operationally Authorised is never granted by class attendance alone—it depends on your employer's supervised onboarding, client-specific rules, and management sign-off.
