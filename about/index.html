The Bureau · Mission & Governance

About the Bureau

Insight Gaps Bureau is an independent forensic data journalism organisation based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. We investigate systemic accountability failures, structural impunity, and public-health contamination.

Our Principle: Evidence Before Narrative

Every story we publish begins with an audited claim register, never with an editorial narrative looking for supporting quotes. Every factual sentence is graded against primary sources: Confirmed, Supported, Contested, or Untraced. Untraced assertions are never published as findings.

Where government agencies, international organisations, and independent watchdogs publish conflicting numbers, we publish the full range and explain the counting choices behind the difference. Where official data has been withheld or fails to exist, we document the absence: an official data void is an investigative finding, not a hole to be filled with guesswork.

Public Data Only

Our reporting is executed entirely from published official records: gazettes, agency registries, parliamentary and ministerial disclosures, court records, peer-reviewed scientific studies, international sensor and satellite archives, and open civic datasets. We do not use RTI petitions, anonymous leaks, confidential interviews, or physical fieldwork in this programme. If a fact cannot be demonstrated from the open record, it is marked as an unverified gap.

Funding & Editorial Independence

Insight Gaps Bureau operates with complete editorial and institutional independence:

  • Zero Grant Interference: We reject all grants, stipends, or donor contracts that mandate physical fieldwork, pre-selected findings, or programmatic policy recommendations.
  • Zero Commercial Influence: We run no sponsored content, no paid tracking analytics, and accept no corporate funding.
  • Self-Sustaining Infrastructure: Site hosting is completely static and lightweight, with zero paid software dependencies.
  • Public Record Exclusivity: By relying strictly on published records, our research cannot be co-opted or suppressed by private interests.

Editorial Structure & Contacts

The bureau publishes in-depth accountability investigations across three primary beats:

  1. Public Transit Safety: Systemic crash counting discrepancies, regulatory capture, and arterial corridor fatalities (Blood Routes).
  2. Judicial Accountability: Attrition rates, procedural statutory delays, and forensic laboratory backlogs in gender-based violence prosecutions (The Impunity Machine).
  3. Environmental Health: Industrial lead contamination, informal battery smelting, and childhood exposure zones across national school boundaries (The Lead Belt).

For research queries, data audit submissions, or formal errata notices, please visit our contact page or review our corrections log.
