About the Bureau

Insight Gaps Bureau is an independent forensic data journalism organisation based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, producing accountability investigations and public-interest data audits.

Our method, in one paragraph: evidence before narrative. Every claim is traced to a named published source, or marked untraced and not published as a finding. Where published series disagree, we show the range and the definitions, not a picked single number. Where data does not exist, we say so — a documented data void is a finding, not a gap to be filled by assertion.

We work from published data only: public bulletins, published datasets, news archives, satellite imagery, and secondary published sources. We do not use RTI requests, interviews, or fieldwork in this programme.

Corrections are a credibility feature: a correction appears dated on the affected story as well as in the corrections log.
