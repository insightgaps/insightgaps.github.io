The Impunity Machine

The Defining Query

Why does Bangladesh's rape justice system produce impunity with the consistency of a machine?

The Data Highlights

310 convictions from 66,711 women across 23 years of One-Stop Crisis Centre records — a 0.46% conviction rate. For the cases that survive, the average wait time is 2,349 days (over 6 years), while the statutory 90-day tribunal limit remains completely compliance-void.

The Forensic Finding

Impunity is not an accident of resource limitations. It is the predictable, documented output of deliberate choices: unpassed witness protection laws, under-resourced forensic DNA labs, and Section 17 legal counter-prosecutions that silence survivors.

View Evidence Export Methodology & Standards → ◯ Live Tracker →
The Institutional Machine: Case & Evidence Flow

The machine relies on strict institutional handoffs. DNA evidence collected at regional One-Stop Crisis Centres (OCC) must be processed at the central National Forensic DNA Profiling Laboratory (NFDPL) before being delivered to the Specialized Tribunal. A parallel track runs through local police under Police Headquarters (PHQ) to submit charge sheets. If any link delays, the trial stalls.
