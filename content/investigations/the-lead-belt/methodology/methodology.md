# BD-INV-003 Methodology

The Lead Belt visual investigation uses Pure Earth / University of Dhaka contaminated-site assessments, UNICEF/BBS MICS 2025 blood-lead figures, OpenStreetMap school proximity analysis, and site-level verification notes embedded in the report dataset.

The map layer uses the report's embedded site dataset and a 500-metre school proximity screen. The dataset download linked from the report exports the mapped site records as CSV for GitHub/static hosting compatibility.

Figures, school counts, distance values, soil lead readings, and verification notes should be updated when the master reporting file changes.
