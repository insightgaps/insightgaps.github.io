Corrections Log

A permanent public record of every factual correction issued by Insight Gaps Bureau. Entries are never deleted — only appended. Speed of correction is a credibility signal, not a liability.

Corrections policy

When an error is identified in a published investigation, Insight Gaps Bureau corrects the record as quickly as possible. The correction is noted directly on the investigation page and added to this log with a timestamp. Rows in this log are permanent — nothing is removed, suppressed, or rewritten. The investigation's status field in the data index is updated to corrected on the same day the correction is published. Corrections are written and approved by the editor — no correction notice is automated.
