AI Use Disclosure

Insight Gaps Bureau uses AI tools at specific, defined stages of research and production. This page is a permanent, public record of which tools are used, what they are permitted to do, and — critically — what they are explicitly prohibited from owning.

Role of AI in bureau operations

AI tools accelerate research, assist with document synthesis, support code production, and provide structured critique. They do not replace editorial judgment. The distinction matters: AI tools handle tasks that benefit from scale and speed; the editor handles every decision that requires accountability.

No investigation is published on the basis of AI-generated claims. No source is assessed by an AI tool. No correction decision is automated. These are not aspirations — they are operational rules enforced at each gate of the investigation workflow.

AI operations table

Every tool in the bureau stack is listed below with its defined role and the boundaries it must not cross. This table is updated whenever the tool stack changes.

Tool Primary Role What It Must Not Own
ChatGPT Strategy, narrative architecture, adversarial critique of published drafts Final editorial decisions; persistent context assumed between sessions
Gemini Pro Long document synthesis, source pattern detection across large corpora Unverified factual claims; final investigation framing
Claude Editorial coherence review, prose clarity, narrative logic, site production Repository management; independent research sourcing
Codex / Claude Code Implementation — component builds, data processing scripts, automation Architecture not yet approved by the editor
Antigravity Agentic multi-step execution, workflow automation Editorial judgment; autonomous publication of any kind
NotebookLM Source vault, citation-grounded research memory across investigation documents Final editorial synthesis; claims without source grounding
Human editor Every final decision at every gate — verification, framing, publication, correction Nothing is delegated at the final gate

What AI does not control

Absolute prohibitions
  • AI tools do not decide whether a claim is verified. The editor verifies against primary sources.
  • AI tools do not assess sources — their reliability, identity, or protection status.
  • AI tools do not decide what is published, when, or under what headline.
  • AI tools do not write correction notices. Corrections are written and approved by the editor.
  • AI tools do not process requests for source removal or source protection decisions.
  • AI tools do not produce the final investigation text without editor review and revision.
  • AI tools do not push to production. The human editor makes every deployment decision.

The underlying principle: AI tools make the editor more capable. They do not substitute for the editor's judgment. The methodology must remain defensible even if every tool in the stack is replaced.
