About the Bureau

An independent forensic investigation bureau, data journalism organization, and operational intelligence practice based in Dhaka, Bangladesh.

Mission

Insight Gaps Bureau exists to produce forensic data audits and environmental risk investigations in the public interest. The bureau's work begins with geocoded coordinates, public records, and legal archives, and ends with published investigations that name what the evidence shows.

Our operating premise is that the most important stories are not in what is declared, but in what the available records have not yet been asked to reveal. Every investigation starts by identifying a gap — between what institutions report and what the raw data confirms.

Our Approach

Every investigation is built on a structured data-verification pipeline to ensure reproducibility and absolute accuracy.

  1. Gate 01 Records Acquiring raw directories, geocoded datasets, and official registries.
  2. Gate 02 Verification Cross-checking coordinates, satellite imagery, and academic sources.
  3. Gate 03 Analysis Applying mathematical models, spatial buffers, and statistical audits.
  4. Gate 04 Publication Releasing the reports alongside raw Excel files and data dashboards.
  5. Gate 05 Corrections Logging corrections and edits permanently on an append-only register.

Editorial Responsibility

The Bureau

Independent editorial desk

Insight Gaps Bureau publishes anonymously so the focus stays on evidence, not individuals. All editorial decisions and final verification are human-owned by the bureau's editorial desk, applying operational-intelligence rigour and forensic data analysis to public-interest journalism.

The desk's practice covers public-records research, KPI and workflow reporting design, exception analysis, spatial dataset mapping, and tracing regulatory failures.

  • Communication & Journalism — university-trained editorial practice
  • Data Journalism Certification — independent press-institute training
  • Digital Verification Certification — international journalism programme
  • Expertise — Operational performance audits, KPI design, spatial data analysis, and geocoded record verification.

Funding

Funding disclosure

Insight Gaps Bureau is completely self-funded by its founder. We accept no political funding, government grants, or corporate sponsorships.

The bureau's commercial analysis track — forensic business intelligence, reporting systems, and operational audits commissioned by corporate clients — generates the revenue that supports our public-interest data investigations.

An absolute firewall separates commercial client engagements from investigative reporting. Clients have no preview rights, influence, or oversight regarding published investigations.

Trust Infrastructure

Credibility requires transparency. To ensure readers can verify and replicate our findings, the bureau maintains a public trust infrastructure:

  • Methodology Philosophy — Details our verification tiers, data handling protocols, and replication instructions.
  • AI Use Disclosure — Lists the specific roles of AI assistants in our research workflow and defines our human-editor verification requirements.
  • Corrections Log — An append-only record documenting every factual correction made to our published investigations.
  • Contact & Tips — Provides channels to securely submit documents, data, or tips to the bureau.
