Corrections Log

A permanent public record of every factual correction issued by Insight Gaps Bureau. Entries are never deleted — only appended. Speed of correction is a credibility signal, not a liability.

Rows in this log are permanent — nothing is removed, suppressed, or rewritten after the fact. The investigation's status is updated to corrected on the same day the correction is published.
