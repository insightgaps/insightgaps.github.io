What you should know before submitting

This form is handled by Formspree. Your submission is sent directly to the editor's email. It is not stored in a database accessible to third parties.

Your identity — if provided — is never stored in the same system as the investigation files. Source identities are replaced with role descriptors in all working documents.

If your situation requires stronger operational security, contact via encrypted email before submitting sensitive documents.
