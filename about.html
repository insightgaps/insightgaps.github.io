About Insight Gaps

An independent forensic data journalism bureau based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. Built to find what the numbers are not being asked to say.

Mission

Insight Gaps Bureau exists to produce forensic data journalism on issues that affect public health, the environment, and institutional accountability in Bangladesh. The bureau's work begins with data — raw, primary, and independently verified — and ends with published investigations that name what the data shows.

The name reflects the operating premise: the most important stories are not in what has been reported, but in what the available data has not yet been asked to reveal. Every investigation starts by identifying a gap — between what institutions say and what the records show, between what policies promise and what the numbers confirm.

Editor

Anowar Hossain Anik

Founder & Sole Editor

Anik is a Business Analyst by profession and a data journalist by practice, based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. He founded Insight Gaps Bureau to apply systematic data analysis to public interest reporting — bringing the same forensic rigour used in business intelligence to accountability journalism.

He holds certifications in Data Journalism from Transparency International Bangladesh (TIB) and in Digital Journalism from Reuters and Meta. His technical work spans Advanced Excel, Google Sheets, Looker Studio, Power BI, Tableau, SQL, and Python.

Funding

Funding disclosure

Insight Gaps Bureau is currently self-funded by its sole editor. No external organisation, donor, government body, or corporate entity funds or influences the bureau's editorial output.

The bureau's analysis track — forensic business reports commissioned by clients — generates income that supports continued investigative work. Commercial analysis work is clearly separated from the investigations track. No client has any editorial input into investigations.

If the funding model changes, this disclosure will be updated immediately.

Methodology

Every investigation published by Insight Gaps Bureau passes through a defined five-gate verification workflow. No claim is published without a traceable primary source. No correction is suppressed. The full methodology — including verification tiers, data handling rules, and AI tool constraints — is publicly documented.
