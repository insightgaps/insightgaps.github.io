One House, Five Hours

One house, born in 1972, carries the entire preservation industry. Over five hours, you track every handoff that keeps her standing.

⚙ FLOWCHART / FIVE-HOUR CURRICULUM ARCHITECTURE · 100% SYNTHETIC
KEY POINT 1

The 1972 Composite House

She is not an abstraction; she is a 1972 suburban home with a real deed, real locks, and real exposure to blight the moment her occupants depart.

KEY POINT 2

The Pre-Inspection Diagnostic

Before touching any lock or tool, the first decision is made in silence: does an empty appearance give you the right to enter? Written, signed, and sealed.

KEY POINT 3

The Five Custodial Hands

Five hands touch one property: Investor, Servicer, National, Office, and Field Crew. When any seam leaks, the job fails.

Governing Rules & Compliance Standards

The Baseline Diagnostic Oath

The pre-inspection decision card must be executed in silence without notes or hints, sealed in an envelope, and retained until Chapter 11.

The Five-Hand Custody Rule

Custodial responsibility passes through five explicit entities; skipping any tier in communication or invoicing constitutes a fatal audit error.

Asset Personification Rule

Always refer to the asset as an integrated physical structure rather than an anonymous work order number.

Operational Requirements & Dealing Protocols

Class File Packet Initialization

Open digital binder containing synthetic deed record, lot boundaries, and preliminary property history.

Silent Decision Protocol

Learner writes one sentence committing to enter or hold, dated and signed.

Dealing Point — First Contact

Never speculate on borrower foreclosure timing with neighbours or municipal inspectors during initial intake.
