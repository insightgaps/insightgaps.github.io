Module 0 · 10 Minutes · The Opening Retelling

Five Hours. One House. The Whole Chain.

1. House → 2. Chain → 3. Order → 4. Work → 5. Evidence → 6. Money → 7. Capstone

Spring, 1972. A new single-family house stands at the end of a suburban street, smelling of fresh Douglas fir framing and cut lawn. For 54 years, through inflation shocks, market booms, and recessions, she held families.

She didn't break on her own. The mortgage loop broke. When the payments stopped and the family drove away, nobody was being paid to care for her — and that is where vacancy decay begins.

1
“The house did not break — the mortgage loop broke.”
A bank owns the financial lien; you safeguard the front door. The gap between Wall Street capital and physical dirt is your job.
2
“Attendance teaches the map; only your employer grants the keys.”
Three separate statuses govern this field. Today builds operational literacy; no classroom certificate authorises you to drill a lock.
3
“Permission first, proof always: done is a claim, verified is the check.”
Visual vacancy is not legal authority to enter; completed work is unpayable without photographic proof. Never paper over a gap.
The Opening Silent Commitment (Exercise X-01) SEALED UNTIL HOUR 5

A neighbor reports a home abandoned. The lawn is three feet high and mail is spilling out. Does visual vacancy give you authority to change the lock?
