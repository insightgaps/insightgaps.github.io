Methodology & Standards

Our reporting follows an adversarial, fail-closed verification architecture. No claim is published because it sounds compelling; it is published only if the underlying data withstands forensic scrutiny.

Ten Core Methodological Rules

  1. Evidence before narrative. Every investigation is built from a structured claim register, never an editorial angle.
  2. One claim, one grade. Every factual claim is individually evaluated and assigned a public grade:
    • Confirmed — Verified by multiple consistent official records or primary datasets.
    • Supported — Backed by a credible single official study, agency release, or established secondary authority.
    • Contested — Disputed by competing official metrics or subject to significant definitional disagreement. Published with full denominator disclosures.
    • Untraced — Assertions lacking verifiable documentation. Stated as documented gaps, never as factual findings.
  3. Ranges, not picked numbers. When official data sources diverge (such as police crash reports versus epidemiological models), we publish the entire range with the exact counting rules for each series.
  4. Public data only. Published ministry reports, agency registries, court proceedings, peer-reviewed scientific literature, and open satellite imagery. We do not use RTI requests, interviews, or fieldwork in this programme.
  5. Secondary computational verification. All statistical findings, rates, multipliers, and spatial buffers must be re-run and verified in an independent computing environment before publication.
  6. Build-time visual integrity. All charts are rendered as clean, build-time SVGs accompanied by comprehensive tabular data inside accessible <details> disclosures. We do not use third-party iframes (Flourish, Datawrapper) or heavy document server embeds.
  7. Satellite corroboration, not proof. Overhead imagery documents structural proximity, roof material characteristics, and temporal physical changes. Satellite imagery cannot measure chemical soil concentration and is explicitly designated as corroboration, never absolute ground truth.
  8. Transparent data voids. When an agency withholds, fails to collect, or removes public datasets, the absence is documented as an investigative finding with an assigned tracking identifier.
  9. Corrections directly on stories. Any corrected fact is documented with timestamp, original text, and amended figure directly on the story page and permanently archived in the public corrections log.
  10. Human accountability. Algorithmic scripts and AI models may assist in syntax checking and data parsing; they are strictly barred from originating claims, drawing causal conclusions, or generating editorial narrative.

Verification Standards & Arithmetic Audits

Before any investigation is staged or published, its numerical claims undergo a fail-closed verification pipeline:

  • Denominator Integrity: A rate without an explicit denominator is invalid. In judicial analysis, court disposal rates are never conflated with conviction rates.
  • De-duplication Audits: Public datasets are audited for synthetic padding, cross-joined duplicates, or corrupted metadata (e.g. camera metadata inserted into date fields).
  • Spatial Verification: Buffer zones (e.g., 500-metre school proximity screenings) are computed using projected local coordinate systems and audited against official upazila boundary shapefiles.

Ethics & Harm Minimisation

Our work adheres strictly to public-interest ethics and legal safeguards:

  • Aggregate Data Protection: On sensitive justice beats, we publish only aggregated counts and court-level metrics. Individual case numbers, survivor identities, or jigsaw re-identification vectors are strictly barred.
  • Zero Proprietary Exposure: The site contains zero real proprietary vendor data, zero private work-order numbers, zero customer addresses, and zero contractor names.
  • Public Official Record Replacement: Rather than relying on subjective pre-publication interviews or unverified right-of-reply exchanges, all findings are grounded strictly in the published official record of courts, ministries, and regulatory gazettes.
  • Trauma-Informed Data Presentation: Human loss is reported with solemnity, precision, and respect, rejecting sensationalist framing.
