Rebuild the Chain Before Moving Forward

"Maple House has survived fifty-four years of American macro-history. She was financed during the record 1972 boom, stood through the subprime flood, and now sits quietly in pre-foreclosure default. But before any contractor turns a drill or bills an invoice, the classroom goes silent. We close every notebook, turn over every sheet, and face the single question: can you draw the chain from memory, or are you operating on a guess?"

KEY POINT 1 • THE FIVE LINKS

One House, Five Hands

Bank → Servicer → National → Our Office → Field Crew. One work order travels all five links before a single lock is changed. If you cannot name who touches the house before you, you do not know whose money you are risking or whose permission you carry.

KEY POINT 2 • THE SYSTEM & THE FILTER

The Boom Built a Filter

Maple House was born in 1972—the biggest total building year in American history (2,379,000 starts). But the same post-war system engineered exclusion through redlining, restrictive deed covenants, and predatory land contracts. Never teach prosperity without the filter.

KEY POINT 3 • CONDITION VS AUTHORITY

Vacant Is Never Permission

A house may look vacant, but condition is not legal authority. Foreclosure is a state legal proceeding, not a cleanout job. Under Washington law in Jordan v. Nationstar, rekeying before foreclosure sale is unlawful possession. Permission first. Proof always.
