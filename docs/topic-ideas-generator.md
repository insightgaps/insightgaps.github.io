**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Focus:** Bangladesh (National & Regional) Forensic Data Journalism  

This document serves as a strategic roadmap and topic generator for future visual and spatial investigations under the Insight Gaps Bureau. Every proposed investigation is designed to follow the strict data standards, evidence tiers, and scrollytelling spec outlined in `docs/governance/`.

---

## 1. INVESTIGATION DESIGN FRAMEWORK
To fit the Insight Gaps portfolio, every new investigation must address:
1. **The Systemic Mechanism:** Move beyond the "accident" or "individual corruption" narrative. Expose the structural incentives (economic, political, regulatory) that make the crisis permanent.
2. **The Data Void:** Explicitly identify and document what the state, corporations, or institutions *refuse to measure or publish* (e.g. absent registers, hidden audits, stalled databases).
3. **The Forensic Scale:** Combine high-resolution spatial maps (Tier 2) or scrollytelling attrition models (Tier 1) to make aggregate statistical tragedies personal and understandable.

## 2. GENERATED TOPIC IDEAS FOR THE FUTURE

### [BD-INV-004] The Air Apocalypse
* **Topic Area:** Environmental Health, Regulatory Capture, Air Quality
* **Investigation Type:** Tier 2 — Visual Spatial Investigation
* **The Defining Query:** Why does Dhaka's air quality remain lethal despite years of court mandates and environmental funding?
* **Data Sources:** 
  - DoE (Department of Environment) brick kiln licensing databases.
  - NASA MODIS/VIIRS thermal anomaly data (detecting active unpermitted kilns).
  - High-resolution school geo-locations mapped against PM2.5 hotspots.
* **The Structural Mechanism:** The illegal expansion of brick kilns into agricultural zones and school buffers, driven by local political shields and a lack of real-time emission tracking.
* **Critical Data Gaps (Voids) to Expose:** 
  - Local air pollution monitoring data per district (held by DoE but unpublished).
  - Health registry of school-age children suffering respiratory illness in kiln zones.
* **Visual Components:** An interactive map of Dhaka's periphery showing brick kiln clusters encroaching on primary school buffers, with scrollytelling showing how prevailing winter winds funnel particulate matter into the urban core.

### [BD-INV-005] The Chemical Maze
* **Topic Area:** Urban Planning, Safety Failures, Industrial Governance
* **Investigation Type:** Tier 1 — Visual Data Investigation (Scrollytelling)
* **The Defining Query:** Seven years after Nimtoli and Churihatta, why do hundreds of chemical warehouses still operate in the dense residential lanes of Old Dhaka?
* **Data Sources:**
  - Fire Service & Civil Defence chemical safety inspection registries.
  - Dhaka South City Corporation trade license approvals.
  - Ministry of Industries relocation site logs (Keraniganj relocation project delays).
* **The Structural Mechanism:** Relocation delays driven by warehouse owners stalling land allocation disputes, combined with split bureaucratic licensing jurisdictions (Explosives Dept vs. City Corporation).
* **Critical Data Gaps (Voids) to Expose:**
  - The master inventory of hazardous substances stored in Old Dhaka residential buildings (withheld from public safety advocates).
  - Real-time licensing status of chemical storage hubs.
* **Visual Components:** A 3D scrollytelling path tracing a single narrow alleyway in Armanitola, illustrating warehouse proximity to residential apartments, electrical transformers, and fire hydrants, showing the compounding probability of disaster.

### [BD-INV-006] The Ghost Fleet
* **Topic Area:** Transport Safety, Regulatory Failure, Waterway Infrastructure
* **Investigation Type:** Tier 1 — Visual Data Investigation
* **The Defining Query:** Why do passenger vessels blacklisted for fatal capsizes routinely reappear under new names on the Meghna and Padma routes?
* **Data Sources:**
  - BIWTA (Bangladesh Inland Water Transport Authority) vessel register.
  - Department of Shipping collision inquiry reports.
  - Historic casualty logs from maritime journalists.
* **The Structural Mechanism:** The "Phoenix rebrand" pattern in inland shipping—owners paint, rename, and obtain secondary registrations for vessels involved in fatal accidents, utilizing political patronage networks.
* **Critical Data Gaps (Voids) to Expose:**
  - Complete passenger manifest logs (refused by operators to avoid over-limit tracking).
  - Vessel stability and hull certification inspection logs.
* **Visual Components:** An interactive scrollytelling flowchart showing how a ship "disappears" in maritime records after an accident and re-emerges with a new registry, showing the structural gaps in shipping licensing.

### [BD-INV-007] The Water Pirates
* **Topic Area:** Natural Resources, Urban Infrastructure, Climate Vulnerability
* **Investigation Type:** Tier 2 — Visual Spatial Investigation
* **The Defining Query:** Who owns the groundwater? Tracking the decline of Dhaka's water table and the unregulated commercial extraction vacuum.
* **Data Sources:**
  - WASA groundwater level monitoring wells.
  - Dhaka WASA deep tube well licensing data.
  - Satellite radar interferometry (InSAR) showing urban land subsidence.
* **The Structural Mechanism:** Commercial extraction by private beverage/real-estate entities using unregulated deep wells, causing depletion that shifts cost to municipal consumers whose shallow wells run dry.
* **Critical Data Gaps (Voids) to Expose:**
  - Metered volume reports from commercial deep tube wells.
  - Quality tests of groundwater in industrial zones.
* **Visual Components:** A 3D spatial model of Dhaka's subsurface, showing the cone of depression expanding beneath key commercial hubs, with layers indicating which residential blocks are losing water pressure first.

### [BD-INV-008] The Paper Hospital
* **Topic Area:** Public Health, Procurement Corruption, Regional Governance
* **Investigation Type:** Tier 1 — Visual Data Investigation
* **The Defining Query:** Why are regional Upazila Health Complexes stocked with millions of dollars in modern medical equipment that sits unpacked and unused?
* **Data Sources:**
  - Ministry of Health procurement registries (equipment purchasing orders).
  - National Audit Department reports on hospital equipment waste.
  - Field verification survey of regional health complexes.
* **The Structural Mechanism:** Procurement-focused corruption where equipment is purchased to generate kickbacks, regardless of the hospital's lack of electricity, technicians, or regional demand.
* **Critical Data Gaps (Voids) to Expose:**
  - National hospital technician vacancy rates by facility.
  - Real-time equipment status registers (withheld to prevent audit trace).
* **Visual Components:** An interactive scrollytelling map of rural clinics, where clicking on a hospital reveals the list of "ghost" equipment (e.g. X-ray machines in crates for 5+ years due to lack of a radiologist).

## 3. HOW TO LAUNCH AN INVESTIGATION
When choosing a topic from this generator, future investigators should run the following checklist:
1. [ ] **Verify the Data Floor:** Can we obtain a baseline dataset (even from secondary NGO/academic sources) to serve as our verified floor?
2. [ ] **Locate the Code ID:** Ensure the investigation is logged under the next ID sequence (`BD-INV-004`, `005`, etc.).
3. [ ] **Identify 3 Key Findings:** Draft the key findings structure early. If they cannot be supported by primary data tiers, pivot the query.
4. [ ] **Design the Infographic Concept:** Sketch the infographic layout early to ensure the data is adaptable for public sharing.
