### System 1: Blood Routes (The Road Transit System — Cause 1 of 3)
- **Role:** *The Immediate Hazard.*
- **Narrative Hook:** ORACLE calculates that as Eid approaches, daily bus lease ("joma") targets force drivers into nocturnal speed-racing. Nusrat faces immediate highway exposure.
- **Empirical Anchors:** 31,578 modelled deaths vs. 5,480 police records (5.8× gap); 351 deaths during 15 Eid travel days; 41.56% nighttime fatality share; BDT 4,000–6,000 fixed daily owner leases; four unpublished crash ledgers.

### System 2: The Impunity Machine (The Criminal Justice System — Cause 2 of 3)
- **Role:** *The Aftermath if Struck.*
- **Narrative Hook:** MUKTI asks: if the highway takes her, what justice will follow? The inquiry reveals a tribunal system engineered for survivor exhaustion rather than accountability.
- **Empirical Anchors:** 310 convictions from 66,711 crisis centre arrivals (0.46%); 78% of survivors arriving after the 72-hour forensic window; 92.3% unprocessed DNA backlog (1,204 stalled samples); 2,349-day average trial wait (26× statutory ceiling); compound punishment certainty of 0.0215% (1 in 4,650).

### System 3: The Lead Belt (The Toxic Environment — Cause 3 of 3)
- **Role:** *The Everyday Chronic Risk.*
- **Narrative Hook:** Even if she avoids the highway and the courts, slow poison surrounds her daily routine: unlicensed battery smelters operate immediately adjacent to residential classrooms.
- **Empirical Anchors:** 59 metres from primary school gate to illegal smelting crucible; peak soil contamination at 680,872 ppm (1,702× safety limit); 65.2% of Dhaka Division children with elevated blood lead; 294 documented contaminated sites across 93 upazilas; 22 years of public awareness with zero certified decontaminations.
