Operating Mandate

Evidence Before Narrative.

Insight Gaps is an independent forensic data journalism desk operating exclusively from public data, official records, remote sensing, and government gazettes.

The 7-Step Forensic Workflow

  1. Hypothesis & Null Hypothesis: Defining testable quantitative questions before touching data.
  2. Dataset Acquisition: Gathering public microdata into immutable data/raw/ archives.
  3. Forensic Cleaning: Documenting every normalization step in a scripted Data Diary.
  4. Independent Bulletproofing: Dual-path recalculation and 25-row random sample auditing.
  5. Published Official Record: Documenting state response via published gazettes and audit reports.
  6. Story-First Publication: Concise 2,000–2,500 word reports with 960px vector graphics and expandable evidence boxes.
  7. Impact & Corrections Tracking: Permanent public correction logging and 30/90/180-day impact audits.
