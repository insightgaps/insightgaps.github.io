# 10-editorial-tone-standards.md — Master Editorial Tone & Style Standards
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  

This document defines the writing style, narrative structure, and ethical limits of using generative AI for copyediting, "humanizing" text, and structuring visual stories within the **Insight Gaps** bureau.

---

## 1. THE INVESTIGATIVE VOICE: FORENSIC MINIMALISM

In forensic data journalism, **the data is the drama**. We do not use emotional adjectives to force a reaction; we let raw facts and clear calculations speak for themselves.

| Avoid Emotional/Vague Language | Use Plain, Factual Evidence |
|---|---|
| "The highway turned into a horrific, blood-soaked war zone during Eid." | "Police logs recorded 43 fatalities on the N1 corridor during the 10-day Eid travel window." |
| "A heart-wrenching tragedy showing complete government neglect." | "The road safety division had no active speed enforcement checkpoints operating past 10:00 PM." |
| "This landmark study conclusively proves corporate corruption." | "The environmental agency's soil analysis showed lead concentrations averaging 820 ppm near the school yard." |

### Core Writing Directives:
1. **Active Voice:** Write in the active voice. Identify who did what (e.g., *"The director signed the license"* rather than *"The license was signed by the director"*).
2. **Short Sentences:** Keep sentences under 25 words. If a sentence contains two ideas, split it.
3. **Proportional Verbs:** Avoid hyperbole. Do not write that a report "demolishes" a claim when it simply "contradicts" it.

## 2. THE AI-SPEAK CHECKLIST: WORDS TO BAN

Generative AI models (like Claude and GPT) share distinct stylistic markers when asked to write or "humanize" text. To maintain a professional, human journalistic style, the following clichés and transitions are **prohibited** and must be stripped by `scripts/research.py --check-ethics`:

* **Prohibited Clichés:** *testament to, beacon, landscape, tapestry, navigate, delve, ultimate, revolutionizing, paramount, nested, deeply, notably, indeed, furthermore, crucial.*
* **Prohibited Framing:** *“It is important to note...”, “In summary, we see...”, “Let us examine the implications of...”* (Journalism does not talk to itself in the text. Jump straight to the statement).

## 3. STORYTELLING STRUCTURES: HOW TO ARRANGE AN INVESTIGATION

We use the **Inverted Pyramid** and **Visual Scrollytelling** models to keep reports readable.

```text
┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
│        1. THE LEAD / MAIN SIGNAL        │ -> The primary quantitative finding.
├─────────────────────────────────────────┤
│        2. SPATIAL & THEMATIC DEEP       │ -> The interactive maps, charts, 
│                   DIVE                  │    and corridor analysis.
├─────────────────────────────────────────┤
│        3. ADMINISTRATIVE MECHANISMS     │ -> The explanation of WHY it happens 
│                                         │    (licensing gaps, lack of patrols).
├─────────────────────────────────────────┤
│        4. METHODOLOGY & DATA DISCLOSURE │ -> The source code, hashes, and 
└─────────────────────────────────────────┘    known data boundaries.
```

1. **The Hook (Visual First):** Start the page with the key visualization (like the Blood Routes national map). The reader should understand the core finding within 10 seconds of landing.
2. **Scrollytelling Mapping:** Keep text segments adjacent to visualizations brief. Each scrolly step must highlight a specific geographic coordinate, corridor, or trend.
3. **The Methodology Anchor:** Every report must conclude with a detailed methodology section. If you excluded data, explain why. If there are margins of error, report them.

## 4. ETHICAL RULES FOR AI WRITING & EDITING

Using AI to assist in structuring or editing is a powerful force multiplier, but it carries severe ethical risks. The following rules are non-negotiable:

### A. The Accountability Rule (Human Final Authority)
* **AI is an Assistant, Not an Editor:** AI can suggest restructuring, check grammar, or shorten paragraphs. You—the human operator—own every single word. Plagiarism, inaccuracies, or false claims generated by AI are your responsibility.

### B. The Proportionality Rule (No Tonal Amplification)
* **Do Not "Sensationalize":** When using AI prompts to "humanize" text, do not allow the system to add emotional adjectives, moral judgments, or speculative claims. The text must remain dry, neutral, and fact-backed.

### C. The Transparency Rule (AI Disclosure)
* **Disclose AI Assistance:** If AI assisted in data analysis, mapping, or structuring the text, the **AI Disclosure Bar** must remain active on the page, linking to the specific methodology log detailing the AI's role.

### D. The Data Security Rule (OPSEC)
* **Do Not Ingest Sensitive Source Data:** Never input unredacted documents, private source names, or confidential transcripts into public or cloud-based AI tools. Protect your source's identity.
