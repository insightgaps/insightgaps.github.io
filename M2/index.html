Five Forces Built the System — and the Filter.

Spring, 1972. A new split-level house stands on Lot 42, smelling of fresh paint and cut Kentucky bluegrass. No one has slept in her yet. No one knows what she will survive. For the next five hours, she carries every rule, photo, and dollar in this class. People protect what they have named — her name will follow her all the way to the day she stands empty.

First — name her or lose her

Before a single lock is changed, decide how to see her. A contractor who sees an anonymous number cuts corners and loses pay. A contractor who sees a home protects what they named.

Her name is Maple House. Remember it — you will protect her, prove her work, and bill her file without losing a dollar.
Three Rules That Carry the Whole Story
01
"No single cause built her — loans, roads, and factory lines built the suburb, but with a filter." Demand + FHA loans + 1956 highways engineered prosperity; redlining and zoning set the fence. Never tell one half without the other.
02
"Say TOTAL for 1972, SINGLE-FAMILY for 2005 — never add the peaks." 1972 was boomer household formation plus federal multifamily subsidies (2,379,000 TOTAL). 2005 was subprime credit and securitisation (1,716,000 SF).
03
"The house did not break; the loop broke — rates and jobs fail before foundations do." At $1,500/month, 2.65% bought $372k in 2021; 7.79% bought $206k in 2023. When rates reset or a factory closes, the payment snaps before paint peels.
